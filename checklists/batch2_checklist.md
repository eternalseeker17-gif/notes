## National Income — Basic Concepts
- [ ] National Income def: total value of goods+services by a country's residents in a year; macroeconomic concept [Parmar]
- [ ] National Income concept first given by Dadabhai Naoroji [Parmar]
- [ ] National Income Committee (1949) headed by P.C. Mahalanobis [Parmar]
- [ ] V.K.R.V. Rao: scientific approach to calculate National Income; gave two-sector economy classification [Parmar]
- [ ] Lucent def: National income = measurement of flow of services/goods in economic system [Lucent]
- [ ] National income vs National wealth: wealth=present assets at a point in time; income=production power over a period [Lucent]
- [ ] NNP at FC = NNP at MP - Indirect Taxes + Subsidies = GNP at MP - Indirect Taxes + Subsidies = National Income [Lucent]
- [ ] National income calculated on 1-year base (captures all seasons) [Lucent]
- [ ] India's National Income data issued by CSO (Lucent, as printed) vs NSO/MoSPI (Lucent §1, as printed) — internal inconsistency, both stated [Lucent — CONFLICT, flag]

## Circular Flow of Income
- [ ] Circular Flow def: continuous movement of goods/services/resources/money in economy [Parmar]
- [ ] Households↔Firms: households provide land/labor/capital → income (wages/rent/profit); firms produce → sell to households; households spend (expenditure) [Parmar]
- [ ] Real Flow def: movement of goods/services/factors of production between households/firms [Parmar]
- [ ] Money Flow def: movement of money/payments between households/firms [Parmar]
- [ ] Factors of Production → Payment table: Land→Rent, Labour→Wages, Capital→Interest, Entrepreneurs→Profit [Parmar]
- [ ] Two-Sector Model: Households+Firms (simplest) [Parmar]
- [ ] Three-Sector Model: +Government [Parmar]
- [ ] Four-Sector Model (Open Economy): +Foreign trade [Parmar]
- [ ] Injection def: funds entering economy, ↑spending/income — eg Investment, Govt Spending, Exports [Parmar]
- [ ] Leakage def: funds exiting circular flow, ↓spending/growth — eg Savings, Taxes, Imports [Parmar]

## National Income Calculation — 3 Methods
- [ ] Value Added/Production/Output Method: value at each production stage; Value Added = Output-Input [Parmar]
- [ ] Worked example: Cotton₹100→Yarn₹200→Cloth₹400→Shirt₹800 (output); value added at each stage = 100/100/200/400 [Parmar]
- [ ] GVA def: value producers add to goods/services bought+sold; GVA=GVA₁+GVA₂+...+GVA_N [Parmar]
- [ ] GVA adopted by CSO in 2015 (as printed; CSO since merged into NSO) [Parmar]
- [ ] Income Method: sum of all factor-of-production income in producing final goods/services [Parmar]
- [ ] Income Method components: (a) Compensation to Employees=wages/salaries; (b) Operating Surplus=profit+rent+interest after deducting employee comp; (c) Mixed Income=self-employed/unincorporated business income (eg doctor's own clinic) [Parmar]
- [ ] Expenditure Method: sum of all spending on goods/services; most common GDP method; = C+G+I+(X-M) [Parmar]
- [ ] GDP (Production/Income Approach) = GVA at Basic Price + Net Taxes on Products [Lucent formula]
- [ ] GDP (Expenditure Approach) = PFCE+GFCE+GFCF+CIS+Valuables+Exports-Imports [Lucent formula]
- [ ] Discrepancy = gap between Production/Income-approach GDP and Expenditure-approach GDP [Lucent]

## Goods Classification
- [ ] Final Goods vs Intermediate Goods table: no further process vs semi-finished/undergo transformation; personal consumption/investment vs inputs for other goods; eg tea bought for personal use=final, tea bought by shop to resell=intermediate [Parmar]
- [ ] 3 fundamental economic problems (scarcity-driven): What to Produce? How to Produce? For Whom to Produce? [Parmar]
- [ ] "What to Produce" = deciding goods/services + quantity, resource allocation [Parmar]
- [ ] Consumer Goods def: finished, direct personal use, not used to produce other goods; eg clothes/food/smartphones [Parmar]
- [ ] Capital Goods def: used by businesses to produce other goods, not immediately consumed; eg machines/factory equipment/tools [Parmar]
- [ ] Inventory def: stock held for sale/production (raw material/WIP/finished goods); sales↑→inventory decumulates, sales↓→inventory accumulates; planned or unplanned [Parmar]
- [ ] Auxiliaries to Trade def: services assisting movement of goods producer→consumer; eg transportation/communication/banking/insurance/warehousing/advertising [Parmar]
- [ ] Stock vs Flow table: static vs dynamic; point-in-time vs over-a-period; eg wealth/loan/inventory/capital/money supply vs income/expenditure/inventory change/depreciation [Parmar]
- [ ] Orange Economy def: creativity/culture/IP-based economy; eg film/music/fashion design [Parmar]

## GDP, GNP, NDP, NNP — Aggregates
- [ ] GDP def: total value of final goods/services within a country's territory in a financial year (Apr1-Mar31); second-hand goods never counted; only final goods counted (avoid double counting) [Parmar]
- [ ] GDP developed by Simon Kuznets (Russian-American economist), 1934 [Parmar]
- [ ] GDP limitations as welfare measure (5): ignores income distribution; excludes non-market activities (household work/volunteering/caregiving); ignores environmental damage; excludes quality-of-life factors (health/education/leisure/safety/happiness); excludes underground/black-market economy [Parmar]
- [ ] GNP def: total value of final goods/services by nationals of a country; GNP=GDP-Factor Income to Abroad+FI from Abroad, OR GNP=GDP+NFIA (Net Factor Income from Abroad) [Parmar]
- [ ] NDP def: economic output after adjusting for depreciation; NDP=GDP-Depreciation [Parmar]
- [ ] Depreciation def: monetary value of asset decreasing over time (wear & tear), seen in capital goods [Parmar]
- [ ] NNP def: monetary value of finished goods/services by a country's citizens in a period; NNP=GNP-Depreciation [Parmar]
- [ ] Market Price vs Factor Cost table: MP includes indirect tax; FC=cost of factors of production, excludes indirect tax [Parmar]
- [ ] FC = MP - Indirect Tax + Subsidy = MP - Net Indirect Tax [Parmar]
- [ ] Basic Price = FC + Net Production Tax; Net Production Tax = Production Tax - Subsidy [Parmar]
- [ ] Product tax (per-unit, eg GST/excise/sales tax) vs Production tax (independent of output, eg land tax/stamp duty/licence fee) — distinction [Parmar]
- [ ] Mnemonic (Hindi): Gross→Net = subtract Depreciation; Domestic→National = add NFIA; MP→FC = subtract Net Indirect Tax [Parmar]
- [ ] Nation's Territory=GDP; Nation's People=GNP; Total Income=Total Expenditure=Total value of goods/services [Parmar]
- [ ] GNI = GDP + Net Primary Income from Rest of World (Receipts-Payments) [Lucent formula]
- [ ] Primary Incomes = Compensation of Employees + Property & Entrepreneurial Income [Lucent formula]
- [ ] NNDI = NNI + other current transfers from RoW, net [Lucent formula]
- [ ] GNDI = NNDI + CFC (Consumption of Fixed Capital) = GNI + other current transfers from RoW, net [Lucent formula]
- [ ] Gross Capital Formation (financing side) = Gross Savings + Net Capital Inflow from RoW [Lucent formula]
- [ ] GCF (expenditure side) = GFCF + CIS (Changes in Stock) + Valuables [Lucent formula]
- [ ] Gross Disposable Income of Govt = GFCE + Gross Saving of Central Govt [Lucent formula]
- [ ] Gross Disposable Income of Households = GNDI - GDI of Govt - Gross Savings of All Corporations [Lucent formula]
- [ ] GVA at basic prices (Production Approach) = Output at basic price - Intermediate Consumption [Lucent formula]
- [ ] GVA at basic prices (Income Approach) = CE + Operating Surplus/Mixed Income + CFC + Production taxes less Production subsidies [Lucent formula]
- [ ] Production taxes/subsidies: independent of output volume; eg Land Revenue, Stamps & Registration, Tax on Profession (taxes); Railways subsidy, village/small industries subsidy (subsidies) [Lucent]
- [ ] Product taxes/subsidies: per-unit; eg GST, Excise, Sales Tax, Service Tax, Import/Export duties (taxes); Food/Petroleum/Fertilizer subsidy (subsidies) [Lucent]
- [ ] "Other Current Transfers" = transfers other than primary incomes [Lucent]
- [ ] GCF estimated via 2 approaches: flow-of-funds (Gross Saving+net capital flow from RoW) OR commodity-flow (by asset type) [Lucent]

## Real vs Nominal GDP, Deflator, Per Capita/Personal Income
- [ ] Real GDP vs Nominal GDP table: base-year reference/constant prices/inflation-adjusted vs current prices/inflation NOT adjusted (higher due to inflation) [Parmar]
- [ ] GDP Deflator = (Nominal GDP/Real GDP)×100; aka "Implicit Price Deflator"/"Price Index" [Parmar+Lucent, same formula]
- [ ] India's GDP calculated by NSO under MoSPI [Parmar]
- [ ] GDP base year = 2022-23 (Parmar, as of 2026) — CONFLICTS with Lucent's tables using 2011-12 base series [CONFLICT, flag both]
- [ ] GDP Price Deflator does NOT factor in import prices; accounts for value of all final goods incl. exports [Lucent]
- [ ] Per Capita Income def: avg money earned per person in an area/period; = National Income/Population [Parmar]
- [ ] Purchasing Power Parity def: goods/services buyable with given money ("common basket of goods") [Parmar]
- [ ] India's global rank (as of 2026, Parmar): 5th in nominal GDP; 3rd in PPP (after USA, China) — CONFLICTS with Lucent's Forbes India (Apr 2025) table showing India 4th nominal [CONFLICT, flag both — cross-referenced with Batch 6's Top-10-economies table]
- [ ] Ranking of GDP (Nominal), Parmar's box: 1.USA 2.China 3.Germany 4.Japan 5.India [Parmar]
- [ ] Personal Income def: total individual income from all sources before taxes [Parmar]
- [ ] PI = NI + Income received but not earned - Income earned but not received, OR PI = NI + Govt transfers + Net interest paid by Govt + Corporate tax payment for social security (garbled as printed) [Parmar]
- [ ] PI = NI + Transfer Payment - Undistributed Corporate Profit; Transfer Payment eg = Govt subsidies/Scholarship/Pension [Parmar]
- [ ] Personal Disposable Income def: money left after taxes+mandatory withholding; = Personal Income - Tax [Parmar]
- [ ] Potential GDP def: total output if resources fully utilised [Parmar]
- [ ] Green GDP = GDP - Environmental Damage [Parmar]
- [ ] Recessionary Gap = Potential GDP - Real GDP [Parmar]
- [ ] Gross Domestic Capital Formation def: total investment to create/increase physical assets; eg factory building, new machinery [Parmar]
