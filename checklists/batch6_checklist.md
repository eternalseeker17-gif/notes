## Poverty — Concept, Types, Estimation (Parmar + Lucent merged)
- [ ] Poverty def: lacking financial resources/essentials for basic living standard; env/legal/social/econ/political causes [Parmar]
- [ ] Types: Absolute(lacking food/water/shelter/clothing/healthcare/education), Relative(income far below society avg), Subjective(feeling poor regardless of income) [Parmar]
- [ ] Poverty Gap Index: measures depth of poverty (extent below line) [Parmar]
- [ ] World Poverty Clock: managed by World Data Lab, real-time global poverty estimates [Parmar]
- [ ] Poverty estimation in India = NITI Aayog's task force, NSSO data under MoSPI; BPL=income/spending below set minimum [Parmar]
- [ ] Dadabhai Naoroji: "Poverty and Un-British Rule in India" — earliest poverty estimate ₹16-35/capita/yr; also first to estimate India's National Income [Parmar]
- [ ] National Planning Committee(1938): set up by S.C.Bose, chaired J.L.Nehru; ₹15-20/capita/month [Parmar]
- [ ] Bombay Plan(1944): Bombay business leaders' proposals; ₹75/capita/yr [Parmar]
- [ ] V.M.Dandekar & N.Rath(1971): first systematic assessment(NSSO-based), expenditure-based; 2250 cal/day rural+urban [Parmar]
- [ ] Alagh Committee(1979): chaired Y.K.Alagh; nutritional+consumption-expenditure basis; Rural 2400cal→₹49.09/mo, Urban 2100cal→₹56.64/mo [Parmar]
- [ ] Lakdawala Committee(1993, printed "Lakadawala"): household per-capita consumption expenditure; CPI-IW+CPI-AL; recommended state-specific poverty lines, calorie basis retained [Parmar]
- [ ] Tendulkar Committee(set up 2005, report 2009): PPP-based(₹33/day); moved away from calorie model; broadened basket incl. private health/education; replaced URP with MRP(30-day food recall/365-day non-food recall); 2011-12: Rural ₹816/mo, Urban ₹1,000/mo; overall poverty 21.9%(Rural25.7%,Urban13.7%) [Parmar]
- [ ] Rangarajan Committee(2012, report 2014): 3 basket components(food/essential non-food/behavioural non-food); incl protein/fat; Mixed Modified Reference Period(weekly/monthly/yearly); Rural ₹972/mo, Urban ₹1,407/mo [Parmar]
- [ ] Trends: 2004-05=407mn poor(37.2% BPL; Rural41.8%,Urban25.7%); 2011-12=270mn poor(21.9%); decline 0.74%/yr(1993-94→2004-05) vs 2.4%/yr(2004-05→2011-12) [Parmar]
- [ ] State-wise(2011-12): highest poverty Chhattisgarh(39.9%)/Jharkhand(37%)/Bihar(33.7%); lowest Goa(5.09%)/Kerala(7.1%) [Parmar]
- [ ] Types of Poor: Usually Poor(normally BPL), Churning Poor(in/out), Occasionally Poor(normally above line) [Parmar]
- [ ] MPI: introduced 2010 by UNDP+OPHI; 3 dimensions(Health-nutrition/child mortality; Education-schooling years/attendance; Living Standards-fuel/sanitation/water/electricity/housing/assets); 10 indicators [Parmar]
- [ ] National MPI(NITI Aayog, Nov2021): 12 indicators(+antenatal care+bank account); declining since 2005-06; 24.82cr escaped multidimensional poverty in 9yrs; UP=most lifted; Bihar=highest MPI, Kerala=lowest MPI [Parmar]
- [ ] HDI: concept by Amartya Sen & Mehboob/Mahbub ul Haq, introduced 1990, score 0-1; Long/healthy life→Life Expectancy Index; Knowledge→Education Index; Decent living→GNI per capita(PPP$)→GNI Index [Parmar]
- [ ] Head Count Ratio(HCR): %population BPL = poverty headcount index = No.of multidim.poor/Total population [Parmar]
- [ ] ILO's 6 Basic Needs: Education, Food, Clothing, Shelter, Health Care, Safe Drinking Water [Parmar]

## Balance of Payments — Structure & 1991 Crisis
- [ ] BoP structure: Current Account(Visible-physical goods/tangible; Invisible-services/intangible; Transfer Payments-unilateral,remittances/aid) + Capital Account(FDI/FPI, External Assistance/Deposits/Borrowings, Other Flows) [Parmar]
- [ ] India = top global remittance receiver, Mexico 2nd [Parmar]
- [ ] FDI: long-term, ≥10% equity in foreign physical asset(eg Walmart-Flipkart); FPI/FII: short-term financial-asset investment,<10% equity(stocks/bonds/MFs/NRI deposits) [Parmar]
- [ ] FDI limit defence sector enhanced 2020: 74% Automatic, up to 100% Government route [Parmar]
- [ ] Current Account Deficit=imports>exports; incl trade balance/net foreign income/foreign aid/net current transfers [Parmar]
- [ ] Twin Deficit=simultaneous current account deficit+fiscal deficit [Parmar]
- [ ] BoP/currency crisis: can't pay essential imports/repay foreign debt; India 1991, PM P.V.Narasimha Rao, FM Manmohan Singh; LPG model response [Parmar]
- [ ] India approached IMF+World Bank for $7bn loan(via IBRD); conditions=SAP(privatisation/trade liberalisation/fiscal discipline/FDI opening) [Parmar]
- [ ] Rupee devalued 2 stages July1991, cumulative~18-20% vs USD; objective=cheaper exports/discourage imports/boost forex inflow [Parmar]
- [ ] Rupee fully convertible on Current Account 1994(RBI, IMF Article VIII); Current Account Convertibility=trade transactions freedom; Capital Account Convertibility=capital transactions freedom(partially convertible in India, RBI-regulated) [Parmar]
- [ ] Tarapore Committee: constituted 1997 by RBI(reviewed 2006), chair S.S.Tarapore; examined CAC feasibility; phased roadmap preconditions=low fiscal deficit/controlled inflation/strong banking(low NPAs)/stable financial system [Parmar]

## Forex Reserves, IMF/World Bank, Exchange Rate Mechanisms
- [ ] Forex reserves: central bank/monetary authority assets, import cover+shock protection; 4 components=FCA, Gold, SDRs("Paper Gold"), Reserve Tranche Position in IMF(higher quota=higher voting power) [Parmar]
- [ ] SDR basket currencies: USD, Euro, Yen, Yuan, Pound [Parmar]
- [ ] Bretton Woods Conference 1944(UN Monetary & Financial Conference): 44 nations,730 delegates→created IMF+World Bank(Twin Orgs), HQ Washington DC; also created IBRD; Bretton Woods system collapsed 1971(US suspended gold convertibility) [Parmar]
- [ ] India ranks 8th in IMF voting power; Top IMF borrowers: Argentina,Egypt,Ukraine,Pakistan,Ecuador [Parmar]
- [ ] Washington Consensus: 10 free-market policy prescriptions(John Williamson,1989) for crisis-prone developing nations(Latin America); promoted by IMF/WB/US Treasury: privatisation/deregulation/trade liberalisation/fiscal discipline [Parmar]
- [ ] IMF major reports: World Economic Outlook, Global Financial Stability Report, Fiscal Monitor, External Sector Report [Parmar]
- [ ] WTO promotes free+fair trade, no arbitrary restrictions(only rule-based protective measures) [Parmar]
- [ ] NOSTRO("our")=domestic bank's foreign-currency account in foreign bank; VOSTRO("your")=foreign bank's domestic-currency account in domestic bank [Parmar]
- [ ] FERA(1973): simplify forex system; violation=criminal offence; abolished(rigid rules hindered growth) [Parmar]
- [ ] FEMA(1999): replaced FERA; enhances forex reserves, promotes trade/payments; violation=civil offence(decriminalised) [Parmar]
- [ ] Depreciation(market fall)/Appreciation(market rise)/Devaluation(govt-induced fall, fixed regime)/Revaluation(govt-induced rise, fixed regime) [Parmar]
- [ ] Depreciation/Appreciation occur under Floating Exchange Rate; fixed rate=pegged rate [Parmar]
- [ ] Lorenz Curve: income/wealth distribution graph, measures inequality; Gini Coefficient 0(perfect equality)-1(perfect inequality) [Parmar]
- [ ] Exchange rate evolution: 1928-48 pegged to Pound Sterling; post-1948(IMF system) pegged to USD(Fixed); 1975 delinked from Pound; 1992 LERMS(dual rate); 1993 fully Market-Determined/Floating [Parmar]
- [ ] NEER: weighted avg vs trade-weighted currency basket, no inflation adjustment; REER: NEER adjusted for inflation, real external competitiveness [Parmar]
- [ ] Currency types: Hard(stable,USD/Euro), Soft(weak/unstable), Hot(moves quickly for short-term profit), Heated(high demand/speculation), Cheap(depreciated), Dear(appreciated) [Parmar]

## Tax Evasion, Money Laundering & Anti-Avoidance
- [ ] BEPS: Base Erosion(shell cos across jurisdictions, ↓taxable profit in high-tax jurisdiction) + Profit Shifting(moving eroded profits to low/no-tax jurisdiction eg Cayman Islands/Bahamas) [Parmar]
- [ ] Round Tripping: domestic black money sent abroad→brought back as FDI via shell cos/tax havens to appear legitimate [Parmar]
- [ ] FATF: HQ Paris, founded July1989(G7); global AML/terror-financing standards [Parmar]
- [ ] 3 Stages of Money Laundering: Placement(illegal funds enter system), Layering(concealing origin), Integration(funds absorbed into economy) [Parmar]
- [ ] Shell Company: paper-only entity, holds assets/routes funds/hides ownership [Parmar]
- [ ] MAT: Section 115JB, Income Tax Act 1961; min tax when normal liability<15% of "book profit"(per Companies Act 2013 calc); applies to all domestic/foreign cos; prevents "zero-tax" profitable cos [Parmar]

- [ ] 1947-1990 trade policy=Import Substitution(inward-looking, protect domestic industry, high tariffs/quotas, avg import duty>125%, govt licence mandatory) [Parmar+B merge][B ★: "closed economy policy", self-reliance via local manufacturing ★]

- [ ] Non-Tariff Measures: anti-dumping measures, Technical Barriers to Trade(TBT), labeling/quality standards [Parmar]
- [ ] Bilateral trade agreement goal: promote free trade between 2 countries(reduce/eliminate customs duties/quotas) [Parmar]
- [ ] FTA(eg BIMSTEC): reduce trade barriers, boost economic cooperation [Parmar]

## Lucent §15 — Trade History & Foreign Trade Evolution
- [ ] Ancient Indian trade highly developed; British East India Co(1600)→trade favoured India till1757; British imposed heavy cloth duty to destroy Indian industry; post-Industrial-Revolution Britain flooded markets w/ cheap goods→colonisation; British cotton monopoly→costlier raw material for Indian weavers→handloom business ruined by1813; late19th century power-machine textile industries emerged [Lucent]
- [ ] Pre-independence trade=colonial principles; post-independence=inward-looking/import-replacement policy [Lucent]
- [ ] India's world-trade share: 1.78%(1950)→0.6%(1995, persisting through1997-98 per ES2001-02); FY2019-20: 18th-largest exporter(1.7% share,INDIA2021), 10th-largest importer(2.6% share,INDIA2020) [Lucent]
