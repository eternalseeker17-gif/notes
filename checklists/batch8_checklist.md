## Millets, PMFBY & Commercial Crops
- [ ] International Year of Millets(IYM): declared by UNGA(75th session, March2021) for 2023; Millets="Smart Food"(high nutrition, climate-resilient, aligned with UN SDGs) [Lucent]
- [ ] PMFBY: launched 2016 Kharif season; comprehensive crop-failure insurance to stabilise farmer income; world's largest crop-insurance scheme by farmer enrolments(~5.5cr applications/yr avg), 3rd-largest by premium received; farmer pays only 1.5%(Rabi)/2%(Kharif) of premium, Centre+State bear rest; 6yrs of implementation: farmers paid ₹25,186cr premium, received claims ₹1.2 lakh cr(as of 31Oct2022) [Lucent]
- [ ] Allied sectors(horticulture/animal husbandry/dairy/fisheries) → improve rural economic conditions+nutrition [Lucent]
- [ ] Commercial crops(def) = produced for trade not self-consumption: Oil-seeds, Sugar crops, Fibre crops, Narcotic crops, Beverage crops [Lucent]
- [ ] ACPC(Agriculture Cost & Price Commission) → decides MSP on govt's behalf [Lucent][DOUBT: Parmar Ch.10 elsewhere in project uses "CACP" naming]
- [ ] MSP(def): price at which govt is ready to purchase crop from farmers directly if market price falls below MSP [Lucent]

## Allied Sectors & NFSA

- [ ] Livestock sector: CAGR 7.9%(2014-15 to2020-21, constant prices); contribution to agri-GVA ↑24.3%(2014-15)→30.1%(2020-21) [Lucent]

## Land Reforms & Land Holdings
- [ ] Land reform measures for reorganising agri land-holding: (1) Land ceiling(max land a farmer can hold, excess=illegal), (2) Chakbandi(aggregating divided/broken land parcels) [Lucent]
- [ ] Land-holding size classification: Marginal(<1 hectare), Small(1-4 hectares), Large(>4 hectares) [Lucent]
- [ ] Chakbandi first implemented in India in 1920, in Baroda [Lucent]

## Agriculture Finance & NABARD
- [ ] Unorganised finance sources: money-lenders, money-dealers, relatives, businessmen, landlords, commission agents | Organised sources: Co-operative Committees, Co-operative Banks, Commercial Banks, RRBs, the Government [Lucent]
- [ ] Co-operative Credit Organisation started first in 1904; Primary Co-operative Committees=short-period credit; State Co-operative Agriculture&Rural Development Banks=long-period credit [Lucent]
- [ ] Land Development Bank established 1919(as Land Mortgage Bank); provides long-term loans [Lucent]
- [ ] NABARD: apex institution of Rural Credit; established 12 July1982 by merging RBI's Agriculture Credit Department + Agricultural Refinance & Development Corporation(ARDC); based on recommendations of Shivraman/Sivaraman Committee [Lucent]
- [ ] NABARD authorised share capital: ₹500cr(original) → ₹5,000cr(w.e.f. 1 Feb2001 amendment) → ₹30,000cr(NABARD Amendment Bill2017, passed January2018; also enabled RBI's exit from NABARD shareholding) [Lucent]

## PYQ-only facts NOT covered above
- [ ] Agriculture Infrastructure Fund(AIF) ★ [B]: supports agri-logistics/cold chains/warehouses/primary-processing units; low-interest loans to farmers/FPOs/cooperative societies/agri-entrepreneurs
- [ ] Green Revolution regional disparity cause ★ [B]: HYV/fertilizer/irrigation access initially limited to well-irrigated regions(Punjab/Haryana/Western UP) → rapid growth there, low benefit in rain-fed/less-irrigated areas → regional imbalance
- [ ] A-R ★ [B]: Green Revolution led to regional imbalances(TRUE) — because HYV tech+irrigation initially available only in select states(Reason TRUE, correct explanation)
- [ ] Structural-transformation failure(1950-1990) ★ [B]: despite agri's declining GDP share, ~65% of population remained agriculture-dependent by 1990(workforce didn't shift to industry/services as theory predicts) — from ~75%(1950) to ~65%(1990) after 4 decades of planning
- [ ] Verghese Kurien ★ [B]: Father of White Revolution in India; led Operation Flood(dairy modernisation, ↑farmer income, milk self-sufficiency); chief architect of Amul model+Anand pattern — NOT the inventor of milk pasteurisation(that was Louis Pasteur, 19th century — tested distractor)
- [ ] Green Revolution 1st phase(mid1960s-mid1970s) effectiveness ★ [B]: highly effective in Punjab/Haryana(canal/tube-well irrigation, wheat-centric, HYV seeds); largely INEFFECTIVE in Bihar(flood-prone, limited irrigation, weak infra, small landholdings→low capital/tech access); Tamil Nadu's impact increased only in later phases(not entirely ineffective)
- [ ] Land reforms(post-independence) key equity goal ★ [B]: abolition of intermediaries(Zamindari system)+land-ceiling laws → ownership rights to actual cultivators; central principle="land to the tiller"(↑productivity+social justice)
- [ ] AGMARK ★ [B]: certification mark for AGRICULTURE products(quality/purity); issued under Agricultural Produce(Grading&Marking) Act, 1937
- [ ] National Mission on Natural Farming ★ [B]: launched November2024; aims chemical-free/natural farming for 1 crore farmers; focus=soil health/↓input costs/↑farmer income/sustainable agri
- [ ] Punjab & Haryana ★ [B]: >85% of net sown area under irrigation(Bhakra Nangal canal system+tubewells are extensive irrigation means)
- [ ] Operation Flood(White Revolution) ★ [B]: launched India January 1970, by NDDB, under Dr.Verghese Kurien's leadership → made India world's largest milk producer
- [ ] Technology Mission on Oilseeds(TMO) ★ [B]: launched 1986 by Ministry of Agriculture(now Agriculture&Farmers Welfare); NDDB given responsibility for oilseed procurement/processing(cooperative model on 'Operation Flood' lines) → launched 'Operation Golden Flow'(self-reliance in edible-oil production, remunerative prices for farmers)
- [ ] ULPIN(Unique Land Parcel Identification Number) ★ [B]: assigns unique ID to every land parcel in India(TRUE); 14-digit alphanumeric ID(TRUE); helps track ownership/resolve disputes(TRUE); used in BOTH rural AND urban areas(NOT urban-only — statement 4 FALSE)
- [ ] Uttar Pradesh ★ [B]: highest proportion of workforce engaged in agriculture(most populous state, fertile Ganga-Yamuna plain, adequate water, majority-rural population)
- [ ] GMO(Genetically Modified Organisms) in agriculture ★ [B]: main purpose=improve crop yield+pest resistance+nutritional quality(via DNA changes) — eg Bt Cotton(pest resistance), Golden Rice(Vitamin-A enriched) — NOT eliminate fertilizers/pesticides entirely or lab-only cultivation(distractors)
- [ ] Occupational/workforce shift from agriculture→industry&services ★ [B] = termed "occupational diversification"(reduces rural-economy risk, generates new income sources, drives structural economic change)
- [ ] Post-independence(~1947) main occupation ★ [B]: Agriculture(~70-75% of population dependent), highest GDP contributor(manufacturing/services sectors still nascent)
- [ ] Fertilizer subsidies(1960-1990) ★ [B]: "double-edged" policy — boosted productivity during Green Revolution BUT benefits mainly reaped by richer/large farmers(small farmers under-benefited due to limited resources) → increased rural economic inequality
- [ ] SMAM(Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization) ★ [B]: FPOs get 75% cost grant for Kisan Drone demonstrations(make modern tech accessible to small farmers, precision agriculture)
- [ ] AMUL milk cooperative ★ [B]: established 14 December 1946, Anand(Gujarat), under guidance of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel; originally "Kaira District Co-operative Milk Producers' Union Limited"; laid foundation of White Revolution by ending middlemen exploitation
