- [ ] World Competitiveness Index: published by IMD(International Institute for Management Development); annual; measures global economic competitiveness [Parmar]
- [ ] World Press Freedom Index: published by Reporters Without Borders(RSF); annual; measures press-freedom level worldwide [Parmar]

## PYQ — HDI, MPI & Related Concepts
- [ ] HDI(def): released by UNDP, first introduced1990(economists Mahbub-ul-Haq+Amartya Sen) ★; 3 dimensions: health(life expectancy at birth), education(mean years of schooling+expected years of schooling), standard of living(per-capita GNI) ★; Expected Years of Schooling = years a school-entry-age child is expected to spend in school given current enrolment patterns ★; Mean Years of Schooling = avg yrs of schooling for adults ≥25yrs ★
- [ ] HDI limitation ★: main limitation=fails to capture social inequality/unequal income distribution within a country(reflects only national averages, not intra-country gaps)
- [ ] "Capability approach" criticism of HDI ★: intangible capabilities(freedom/self-respect/life-choices) are difficult to measure/operationalize, so HDI stays limited to narrow indicators(health/education/income)
- [ ] A-R ★: HDI can increase even if income drops(TRUE) — because improvements in life expectancy&education can offset income decline, since HDI=average of 3 dimensions(Reason TRUE, correct explanation)
- [ ] A-R ★: high HDI country may still have high multidimensional poverty(TRUE) — because HDI is an average, doesn't capture intra-country inequality(Reason TRUE, correct explanation)
- [ ] A-R ★: rise in GNI per capita ALONE cannot significantly improve HDI even with falling life expectancy/education(TRUE — HDI needs balance across all 3 dimensions, not income alone) — a "false" statement variant tests this the other way
- [ ] A-R ★: India's HDI improved significantly post-2010(TRUE, growth in education/health/per-capita income) — Reason: methodology revised to include Inequality-adjusted HDI(IHDI) in 2010(TRUE, but NOT the correct explanation — actual improvement driven by domestic development/social-welfare schemes, not the methodology revision)
- [ ] Statement check ★: HDI is NOT based on life expectancy+standard of living ONLY(it also includes education) — statement 1 INCORRECT; Life expectancy at birth IS used for HDI(statement 2 CORRECT); MPI does NOT measure health+education ONLY(also standard of living) — statement 3 INCORRECT; Child mortality+nutrition ARE MPI health indicators(statement 4 CORRECT) → only statements2&4 correct
- [ ] "Human Development Ethics" critique of HDI ★: argues development shouldn't be limited to quantitative/statistical data alone, should incorporate cultural diversity+ethical values — challenges HDI's implicit assumption of universal Western values/development goals
