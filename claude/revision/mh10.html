3Miscellaneous — Colonial Architecture, Women Achievers & Grab-bag Facts

3.1 Colonial architecture

  • ★ French colonial architecture (Puducherry/Pondicherry's "White Town") — wide verandas, tall doors/windows, sloped tiled roofs, European classical influence adapted to tropics; NOT Mughal domes/arches; a distinctive "Franco-Tamil" blend (French classical + local Tamil).
  • ★ Churches of Old Goa (Basilica of Bom Jesus, Sé Cathedral) — Portuguese Baroque & Manueline styles.
  • ★ Notre Dame des Anges Church, Puducherry — French/Greco-Roman style; Doric & Corinthian columns give a "transparent enclosure" façade.
  • ★ British colonial architecture used lime mortar + large foundation platforms, enabling arches/domes/colonnaded verandas in the Indo-Saracenic style (a separate feature, not a direct consequence of the foundation technique).
  • ★ Rashtrapati Bhavan ("Viceroy's House") — planned after the 1911 Delhi capital-shift decision; construction began 1912, completed 1929; architect Edwin Lutyens.
  • ★ Victoria Memorial, Kolkata — built 1906–21 under Curzon, in memory of Queen Victoria.

3.2 Women achievers/firsts

  • ★ Sucheta Kripalani — 1st woman Chief Minister of any Indian state (UP, 1963–67).
  • Aruna Asaf Ali — 1st Mayor of Delhi. Anna Chandy — 1st woman judge of India.
  • ★ Debala Mitra — 1st woman Director-General, Archaeological Survey of India (1981–83); excavated the Diamond Triangle, Odisha (Ratnagiri/Udayagiri/Lalitgiri).
  • ★ National Women's Day, 13 February — Sarojini Naidu's birth anniversary.
  • ★ Parbati Giri (Sambalpur, Odisha) — "Mother Teresa of Western Odisha," freedom fighter/social reformer.
