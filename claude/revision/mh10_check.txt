Pingali Venkayya/flag design, Thakkar Bapa's biography, C.R. Das's book, land-reform intermediary-

highest share of book-external, current-affairs/general-knowledge content.

A.3 Cross-book & cross-source doubts carried forward
  Widow Remarriage Act's viceroy: T5 credits Canning (matching Lucent's own Governors-General
  list); Parmar's socio-religious and Governors files put the Act under Dalhousie. Three-way restatement
  of the same unresolved doubt (cross-ref Ch.2, Ch.9).
  Simon Commission's dyarchy recommendation: T5 says it "recommended dyarchy in provinces";
  Parmar's CDM file says it recommended "abolition of dyarchy" — a direct, unreconciled contradiction
  (cross-ref Ch.7).
  1886 Calcutta session delegate count: T7 says 436; Parmar's INC file says 434 (cross-ref Ch.4).
  T1–T4's quotation/attribution wording is transcribed as printed by Lucent; the raw extraction itself
  flags that these are not independently verified against a primary source, and neither am I.
