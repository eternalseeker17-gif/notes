British Parliament's 1839 Draft Act to regulate/abolish slavery: Bombay denied the need for any new
law; Madras questioned the Act's utility/expediency — both skeptical/protective, neither supportive. This
entire episode (the 1839 anti-slavery draft act and the two Presidencies' responses) does not appear
anywhere in the Modern History raw extraction; sourced to the PYQ book's own explanation text alone.
