 2 Swadeshi Movement & the 1905–07 Congress Sessions
  ★1905 Benaras (Gokhale): approved Swadeshi/Anti-Partition; formal Swadeshi proclamation, 7 Aug
  1905, Calcutta Town Hall.
  1906 Calcutta (Naoroji): 4 resolutions — Swaraj (goal), Boycott, Swadeshi, National Education.
  ★1907 Surat (Rash Behari Ghosh): Congress splits Moderates/Extremists over how far to extend the
  anti-Partition agitation. British "Carrot and Stick": appeased Moderates, suppressed Extremists (Tilak
  sent to Mandalay Jail 6 yrs; Aurobindo retired to Pondicherry; Lajpat Rai left for Britain).

Lucknow Session, 1916 (Ambika Charan Mazumdar): reunion of Moderates & Extremists; Lucknow

electorate; joint demand for "dominion status." INC's own demands: self-government, abolish India
Council, separate judiciary/executive, Secretary of State's salary off the Indian treasury, half the
Executive Council Indian, 1/3rd Muslim representation.

transfer of government control to Indians, gradual development of local self-government.
