A.3 Cross-book & cross-source doubts carried forward
  Jallianwala Bagh death toll: official 379 in both; Parmar's "actual over 1000" vs Lucent's ">1200
  wounded" (not the same metric — deaths vs wounded — transcribed as printed, not merged).

  March 1940."
  Non-Cooperation approval: Parmar says Calcutta special session (1920) approved it, "later
  endorsed" at Nagpur; Lucent's own wording differs slightly in emphasis but agrees on both sessions —
  no real conflict.
  Harijan's founding date: Parmar's Gandhian-file box dates the Gujarati Navjivan to 1919; Lucent's
  "As an Editor" list separately dates Harijan itself to 1919–31 — two different papers under similar-
  sounding names, not merged.
