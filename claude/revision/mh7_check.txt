 2 Kakori, Lajpat Rai's Death & the Lahore Conspiracy Case
  Kakori Train Robbery (1925), near Lucknow — hanged: Ashfaqullah Khan, Roshan Singh, Ram
  Prasad Bismil, Rajendra Lahiri (renamed "Kakori Train Action" by UP govt., 2021).
  1928: Lala Lajpat Rai, protesting Simon Commission ("Simon Go Back"), fatally struck by police officer
  Scott during a lathi charge. To avenge his death, ★Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev & Rajguru killed
  Saunders (mistaken for Scott), Lahore.
  8 April 1929: Bhagat Singh & Batukeshwar Dutt bombed the Central Legislative Assembly (protesting
  the Public Safety Bill 1928 & Trade Disputes Bill 1929); surrendered deliberately — "not to kill but to
  make deaf hear."
  Hunger Strikes (1929–30): demanded political-prisoner status; Jatindra Nath Das died after a
  63-day fast, 13 Sep 1929.
  Lahore Conspiracy Case: special 1930 ordinance let the trial proceed in the accused's absence;
  approvers Hans Raj Vohra, Jai Gopal, Phanindranath Ghosh, Sobha Singh. ★ Bhagat Singh, Sukhdev &
  Rajguru hanged 23 March 1931 (a day early) — now Shaheed Diwas. Slogan "Inquilab Zindabad"
  (coined by Hasrat Mohani, 1921; popularised by Bhagat Singh). Bhagat Singh wrote Why I Am an
  Atheist; Durgawati Devi ("Durga Bhabhi") was a prominent HSRA member.
  1930, Chittagong Armoury Raid — Surya Sen ("Master Da"); hanged Jan 1934; women participants
  Pritilata Waddedar, Kalpana Dutta, Suniti Choudhury, Bina Das, Shanti Ghosh.
  Chandrashekhar Azad shot himself, Alfred Park, Allahabad, 27 Feb 1931 (now Chandrashekhar Azad
  Park).

 4 Lahore Session 1929 & the Road to Civil Disobedience
  Irwin's Declaration/Deepavali Declaration (1929): promised future Dominion Status + a Round
  Table Conference.
  Lahore Session, Dec 1929 (Jawaharlal Nehru): Purna Swaraj adopted as the goal; tricolour hoisted
  on the Ravi's bank, 31 Dec 1929 ("Inquilab Zindabad"); 26 Jan 1930 fixed as the first Independence
  Day; Congress authorised CDM; decided to boycott the 1st RTC.
  Gandhi's 11 demands (ultimatum, ignored by Irwin): arms licences, 50% cut in military spending,
  release political prisoners, favourable rupee-pound rate, abolish the Salt Law, 50% cut in civil-service
  salaries, reserve coastal shipping for Indians, import duty on foreign cloth, reform/abolish the CID,
  reduce land revenue, total prohibition.

  HOOK   Dominion Status was first demanded by Tej Bahadur Sapru & M.R. Jayakar.

Gandhi-Irwin Pact/Delhi Pact, 5 March 1931 (Sapru & Jayakar brokered): Congress suspends CDM,
joins 2nd RTC. Accepted: release of non-violent political prisoners, return of unsold confiscated property,
salt-production rights for personal use. Not accepted: public inquiry into police excesses, commutation of
Bhagat Singh/Sukhdev/Rajguru's death sentences. Nehru's remark: "This is the way the world ends, not
with a bang, but a whimper."
Karachi Session, 29 March 1931 (Sardar Patel) — Gandhi greeted with black flags over Bhagat Singh's
execution; ratified the Gandhi-Irwin Pact; CDM suspended; Purna Swaraj reiterated (with its meaning
formally stated for the first time); adopted Fundamental Rights & National Economic Policy resolutions.
2nd RTC's failure → Congress resumed CDM (29 Dec 1931); Viceroy Willingdon refused to meet Gandhi;
Gandhi arrested 4 Jan 1932; CDM finally withdrawn April 1934.

A.2 New facts sourced only from the PYQ book (not in Parmar or Lucent)
The Writers' Building raid (8 Dec 1930, Benoy Basu/Badal Gupta/Dinesh Gupta, killing of N.S. Simpson,
and the renaming of Dalhousie Square to B.B.D. Bagh) does not appear anywhere in the Modern History
raw extraction — it sits chronologically between the Lahore Conspiracy Case (1930-31) and Chittagong
(1930) but neither source book names this specific event; sourced here entirely to the PYQ book's own
explanation text.

A.3 Cross-book & cross-source doubts carried forward
  Lajpat Rai's death / Saunders murder: Parmar names police officer "Scott" as who struck Lajpat Rai
  and says Saunders was killed "instead of Scott" (mistaken identity); Lucent names no "Scott," saying
  only that Bhagat Singh's group killed "a white police official, Saunders."
  Simon Commission's appointment vs arrival date: Parmar gives 1928 as the arrival year;
  Lucent/PYQ book both confirm appointment Nov 1927, arrival/Bombay landing Feb 1928 — not a real
  conflict, just different reference points.
  Dandi March distance: Parmar 241 miles vs Lucent 240 miles — not reconciled.
  Gandhi's arrest date (Dandi era): Parmar 4 May 1930 vs Lucent 5 May 1930.
  Gandhi's 1932 arrest date: Parmar's "4 Jan 1932" arrest vs Lucent's chronology listing "Jan 3, 1932"
  as the second CDM phase's start — likely the same event described from two angles, not reconciled.
  "Simon Go Back" coinage: Parmar attributes the term to Yusuf Meherally, who is credited elsewhere
  (Lucent, cross-ref Ch.8) only with coining "Quit India" — a probable over-attribution, left as printed.

A.4 Completeness check
Every fact in Parmar's ch.7 raw extraction (Socialism/CPI/revolutionary tables, Simon Commission, Nehru Report, Bardoli,
factors leading to CDM, Dandi/Dharasana, Gandhi-Irwin Pact, Karachi, RTCs) is represented above, along with the
corresponding portion of Lucent's continuous Gandhian-Era narrative (Simon Commission through the 3rd RTC) that the
raw extraction's own Part B index points to. Deferred to Ch.8 (Quit India & Independence), per the raw extraction's own
C6 cross-reference: the Communal Award, Poona Pact and Government of India Act 1935, which chronologically follow
the 3rd RTC and are Parmar's ch.8 content rather than ch.7's.
