1Inflation Basics

Inflation = sustained rise in general price level → purchasing power falls. During inflation, the debtor/borrower benefits over the lender (repays with money worth less) ★ — when the price level rises, the value of money falls ★ (a directly tested macro-concept statement). Real Interest Rate = Nominal Interest Rate − Inflation.

HOOK Fisher's Quantity Theory (Irving Fisher, also gave "Money Illusion"): MV = PT — M=Money Supply, V=Velocity, P=Price Level, T=Transactions. ↑M → ↑P (assuming V,T constant) → inflation, purchasing power ↓.
TypeRange

Demand-Pull Inflation ★Cost-Push Inflation
Caused by ↑demand (strong growth, ↑govt spending, ↑exports); "too much money chasing too few goods" ★ — govt ↑spending/↓taxes → ↑aggregate demand → firms can't scale production fast enough → shortage → prices rise ★Caused by ↑production costs (wages, raw materials, indirect taxes); supply-side, can be long-term
TRAP Stagflation ★ = high inflation + stagnant/falling growth + high unemployment simultaneously — a "lethal combination," makes anti-inflation policy risky (raises unemployment further) ★. NOT the same as Recession, Hyperinflation, or Deflation (tested distractors).

WPI weights: Manufactured goods 64.2%, Primary articles 22.6%, Fuel & power 13.15%. CPI weights: Food & Beverages 45.8% (highest), Housing 10%, Fuel & power 6.8%. MPFA (Feb 2015), GoI-RBI agreement: RBI to maintain CPI inflation at (4±2)%. Core Inflation = headline inflation excluding food & energy.

HOOK Decline in core inflation globally in 2024 ★ mainly due to a drop in international commodity prices ★ (energy/metals/agri-input costs fell, easing "imported inflation") — not export-demand or fiscal-deficit factors.
HOOK Abhijit Sen Committee ★ reviewed/reformed the WPI base year and methodology; report submitted in 2008 ★, recommended base-year shift from 1993-94 to 2004-05.
TRAP Disinflation = inflation rate falling but prices still rising (e.g. 10%→8%→7%→5%) — NOT the same as Deflation (an actual fall in the price level, purchasing power rises). Phillips Curve = inverse unemployment-inflation relationship (high inflation ↔ low unemployment and vice versa).

3Fiscal/Monetary Response to Recession & Inflation

HOOK Keynesian prescription for a recession ★ (tested repeatedly across shifts): increase government spending, cut taxes ★ — "expansionary fiscal policy," stimulates aggregate demand to compensate for weak private investment. NOT raise interest rates, promote imports, or reduce spending.

6Migration & Destination-Region Challenges

HOOK Unskilled rural-to-urban migration ★ → migrant labourers forced into low-wage informal-sector work without social security → exploitation of migrant labour + informal-sector growth ★ = the tested "challenge for destination regions" (not increased per-capita income or reduced unemployment — tested distractors). Push factors (poverty, unemployment, war) force people out; Pull factors (jobs, opportunities) draw them in ★ — both operate together in real migration decisions.

7Poverty Measurement

C. Rangarajan Committee ★ — constituted by the Planning Commission in 2012 ★ to review poverty-line methodology; submitted report 2014, revised the Tendulkar Committee's estimates, proposed monthly per-capita expenditure of ₹972 (rural)/₹1407 (urban) as the poverty line.

8Employment & Welfare Schemes (Lucent §7 catalogue)

SchemeKey facts
MGNREGA ★Started as NREGA (Act passed 7 Sept 2005, in force 2 Feb 2006); renamed MGNREGA 2 Oct 2009 ★. Guarantees 100 days of unskilled manual wage employment/year to rural households ★ (work in casual manual labour at statutory minimum wage; work must start within 15 days, within 5 km; unemployment allowance if not provided). Centre bears 75%, States 25% of material/skilled-wage costs.
PM Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) ★Launched 28 Aug 2014 ★, financial-inclusion mission — zero-balance savings account ★ for every household, RuPay card with ₹1 lakh accident cover; implemented by Dept. of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance ★ (completed 10 yrs, Aug 2024).
Atal Pension Yojana (APY) ★Launched 9 May 2015 (completed 10 yrs May 2025); for unorganised-sector workers ★, age 18-40 years ★ eligible, fixed monthly pension ₹1,000-5,000 from age 60.
Stand-Up India ★Launched 5 April 2016 — promotes entrepreneurship among SC/ST and women ★; mandates every bank branch to lend ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore to ≥1 SC/ST borrower + ≥1 woman borrower for Greenfield enterprises.
PM Vishwakarma Scheme ★Launched 17 Sept 2023 (Vishwakarma Jayanti); traditional artisans/craftspeople — ₹500/day stipend during training ★, toolkit e-voucher ₹15,000, collateral-free credit up to ₹1 lakh (then ₹2 lakh) at 5% concessional interest.
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) ★Launched 1 May 2016, Ballia (UP), by Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas — deposit-free LPG connections to women of BPL households ★; targeted 5 crore connections; Ujjwala 2.0 (2021) added 1 crore more.
DAY-NRLM ★Launched June 2011 (as NRLM/erstwhile SGSY), Ministry of Rural Development — empowers rural women via Self-Help Groups (SHGs) ★, financial inclusion, skill development.
Beti Bachao Beti Padhao ★Launched 22 Jan 2015, Panipat, Haryana — addresses declining child sex ratio, girl-child survival/education/protection ★ (not women's health insurance or marriage support — tested distractors).
Digital India ★Launched 1 July 2015 ★ — theme "Power to Empower"; 3 pillars: digital infrastructure as a utility, governance/services on demand, digital empowerment of citizens.
MUDRA / PMMYLaunched 2015; collateral-free credit up to ₹10 lakh via SIDBI's MUDRA Ltd. 3 categories: Shishu (≤₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,000-5 lakh), Tarun (₹5-10 lakh).
DOUBT One PYQ (bucket 16 Q89 explanation) states MGNREGA was renamed "Viksit Bharat – Ram Ji" in Dec 2025 with guaranteed days raised to 125 — this postdates the raw extraction (which only knows the 2009 MGNREGA renaming and 100-day guarantee) and is not independently verified; flagged as an unconfirmed claim inside the PYQ book's own explanation text, not carried into the main body above.
