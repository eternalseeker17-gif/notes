1Inflation — Basics

  • Inflation → ↑general price level of goods/services → purchasing power decreases
  • During inflation → debtor/borrower benefits more than lender (repaid money worth less than when borrowed)
  • Real Interest Rate = Nominal Interest Rate − Inflation
  • Fisher's Quantity Theory: MV=PT (M=Money Supply, P=Price Level, V=Velocity, T=Transactions) → M↑→P↑ (V,T constant) → direct M-inflation link
  • Irving Fisher → gave "Money Illusion" concept
  • Seigniorage → central bank's profit from currency issue = Face Value − Production Cost

Both preserved verbatim, essentially the same concept stated twice.

3Measuring Inflation — WPI, CPI & Related Indices

WPICPI
Wholesale-level, pre-consumer prices; published by Office of Economic Adviser, Min. of Commerce & Industry; base year 2022-23 (prev. 2011-12); monthly (14th)Consumer/retail-level, cost-of-living; released by NSO/MoSPI; base year 2023-24 [see DOUBT]; monthly (12th)
Weights: Manufactured 64.2%, Primary articles 22.6%, Fuel&Power 13.15%Weights: Food&Beverages 45.8% (highest), Housing 10%, Fuel&Power 6.8%

Both formulas: Index = 100 × (current-year basket cost / base-year basket cost). Inflation Rate = [(Current Index − Previous Index)/Previous Index] × 100.

DOUBT Parmar states CPI base year as 2023-24 on p.266, then as 2024 for "CPI (Rural/Urban/Combined)" on p.267 — internal inconsistency in the same book, both preserved.

4 types of CPI: CPI-IW (base 2016), CPI-AL (base 2019), CPI-RL (base 2019) — all released by Labour Bureau; CPI (Rural/Urban/Combined, base 2024, by NSO/MoSPI) — used by RBI for inflation targeting.

PPI (Producer Price Index) → seller's-perspective price change (vs CPI = buyer's perspective); captures "factory gate" prices before tax/distribution costs; a leading indicator (cost passed to consumers later).

MPFA (Monetary Policy Framework Agreement) → signed GoI+RBI, Feb 2015; mandates RBI maintain price stability + CPI inflation target of (4±2)%.

Core Inflation → excludes food+energy prices (via CPI); = Headline Inflation − Volatile Inflation.

IIP → industrial-production growth indicator; base year 2022-23; by NSO/MoSPI. 8 core industries = 40% of IIP weight, decreasing order: Refinery Products → Electricity → Steel → Coal → Crude Oil → Natural Gas → Cement → Fertilizers.

5Unemployment — NSSO Measurement Methods

  • Unemployment (Parmar) → actively seeking job, unable to find work
  • NSSO's 3 methods: Usual Status, Current Weekly Status, Daily Status
MethodDetail
Usual Status (US)Reference: last 365 days; identifies chronic/long-term unemployment; 2 components — Principal Status (PS): majority-time activity in the year; Subsidiary Status (SS): ≥30 days/yr additional activity apart from PS; combined = "Usual Status (PS+SS)"
Current Weekly Status (CWS)In labour force last 1 week; employed if worked ≥1hr on any 1 day
Current Daily Status (CDS)Measures unemployment intensity; in labour force 1 day: >4hrs=100% engaged, 1-4hrs=50% engaged, <1hr=unemployed

LFPR = % of population in labour force (working/available) | WPR = % of population employed | UR = % unemployed among labour force.

TRAP Elderly Dependency Ratio ★ [B] = ratio of elderly (65+) to working-age population (NOT children-to-working-age, elderly-to-children, or urban-rural elderly ratio — tested distractors ★).

6Types of Unemployment (Parmar + Lucent, merged)

TypeMeaning & Example
StructuralSkill mismatch vs job requirements; eg tech replacing farm labour
EducatedDegree-holders unable to find work (graduate unemployment); urban India; eg engineering grad lacking practical skills
FrictionalShort-term, job-search driven (Search/Voluntary unemployment); eg switching jobs in cities; [Lucent's phrasing: new industries drive out old ones, workers change over]
Disguised / Under-employmentMore workers than needed, removal wouldn't hurt productivity; eg farm family of 5 needing only 3; [Lucent: person contributes less than capable of, eg engineer working as clerk]
CyclicalLabour force reduced due to business cycles; opportunity returns when economy revives; mostly urban; eg Great Recession [Lucent: "result of depression in an economy"]
SeasonalUnemployment at specific times of year; mostly rural [Lucent: farmers/farm labourers between harvest and next sowing]; eg firework sellers off-season
Classical (Real Wage)Real wage too high → more job-seekers than openings; eg workers demand more than company can pay
NaturalLowest unemployment level a healthy economy sustains without causing inflation
Open Unemployment [Lucent, new]Able+willing but NO work at all available
Compulsory Unemployment [Lucent, new]Willing to work at the current (prevailing) rate but simply can't get work

Great Recession def: prolonged downturn ≥2 consecutive quarters, eg US Subprime crisis; a prolonged recession = Depression, eg Great Depression USA 1929-1939.

Marginal Worker: employed <6 months/183 days; eg landless agri labourers, low-wage industry workers. Main Worker: employed >6 months/183 days; eg construction workers, seasonal employees, independent contractors.

Unemployment in India (Lucent) = structural (capital/resource shortage), NOT Keynesian demand-deficiency. Main causes: slow economic development, population explosion, outdated technique, improper education system, limited govt-planning effect.

  • NSSO's own 3 concepts (Lucent's distinct wording): General Status (>1yr unemployed, long-term); Weekly-unemployment (no work even 1hr/week); Daily unemployment (considered the "best" concept)
  • Ninth Plan: 3.6 crore fresh unemployed began seeking jobs

10MGNREGS / NREGA — Full Detail

  • NREG Bill passed Parliament 7 Sept 2005; Presidential assent same year → NREGA
  • Act provides ≥100 days employment/yr to 1 able-bodied person per rural household
  • Came into force 2 Feb 2006; initially 200 districts → now covers all 740 rural districts
  • Renamed MGNREGA — 2 Oct 2009
  • Cost sharing: Centre 75% + State 25% (material + skilled/semi-skilled wages)
  • FY2025-26 wage hike: 2.33%-7.48% range, ₹7-₹26 increase; ₹7 hike in 5 states; highest ₹26 in Haryana (→₹400)
HOOK Employment Guarantee Act 2005 — full 9-point features: (1) ≥100 days/yr guaranteed casual manual labour at statutory min wage, paid within 7 days; (2) work provided within 15 days of demand, within 5km; (3) unemployment allowance (≥1/3 min wage) if work delayed; (4) medical treatment/hospitalisation + daily allowance ≥half min wage for injury; ex-gratia to legal heirs on death/disability; (5) 5% wage deduction possible for welfare schemes (health/accident/survivor/maternity insurance); (6) strict penalties for non-compliance; (7) full transparency/public scrutiny of accounts; (8) District Collector/CEO responsible at district level; (9) Gram Sabha monitors Gram Panchayat via social audit.

11Development & Employment Programmes — Full Detail

AIM (Atal Innovation Mission) → NITI Aayog flagship, promotes innovation/entrepreneurship; umbrella org aligning Central/State/sectoral innovation policies.

PMKVY → Union Cabinet cleared 21 Mar 2015; skill training for 1.4 million youth, ₹1,120 crore outlay; via Min. of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship + NSDC; targets Class-X/XII dropouts/fresh labour-market entrants; 1.56 crore trained since 2015 (till 8 Oct 2024), 54% aged 15-29.

ABRY (Aatmanirbhar Bharat Rojgar Yojana) → part of AB 3.0 package, post-COVID employment recovery/incentive; via EPFO; till Mar 2024: 1,52,517 establishments claimed ₹10,188.50 crore benefits for 60.49 lakh beneficiaries.

PAHAL (DBTL) → launched 1 June 2013, 291 districts; relaunched 15 Nov 2014 (54 districts) + 1 Jan 2015 (rest of country); transfers LPG subsidy directly to 15crore+ consumers' bank accounts; Guinness World Record — largest DBT scheme.

Beti Bachao Beti Padhao → launched 22 Jan 2015, Panipat, Haryana, by PM; addresses gender imbalance/discrimination; Mahendergarh+Jhajjar (Haryana) = worst sex ratio (775 girls:1,000 boys, lowest in India); Haryana CM's own scheme = "Aapki Beti Humari Beti".

Sukanya Samriddhi Accounts → interest 9.1% p.a. (2014-15), yearly compounded; min ₹1,000/max ₹1,50,000 per FY, multiples of ₹100, lump-sum allowed, no deposit-count limit; opens only ≤age 10 (from DoB); closes after 21 years.

Atal Pension Yojana → launched 2015, targets unorganised-sector workers w/o statutory social security; via National Pension System (NPS), administered by PFRDA.

PMJDY → National Mission on Financial Inclusion, announced 2014; objectives: universal banking access (branch/BC within reasonable distance) + ≥1 basic bank account/household w/ RuPay Debit card (₹1 lakh accident insurance); slogan "Mera Khata – Bhagya Vidhaata"; accident cover via HDFC Ergo; life insurance ₹30,000 via LIC.

PMMY → launched 2015, administered by MUDRA Ltd (SIDBI subsidiary); collateral-free credit up to ₹10 lakh; 3 categories — Shishu (≤₹50,000), Kishore (₹50,000-5,00,000), Tarun (₹5,00,000-10 lakh).

Stand-Up India → launched 2016; promotes SC/ST+women entrepreneurship; bank loans ₹10 lakh-₹1 crore, ≥1 SC/ST borrower + 1 woman borrower per Scheduled Commercial Bank branch, for Greenfield enterprises (trading/mfg/services/allied-agri); extended 2019-20 to align with 15th FC period (2020-25); margin money ≤15% of project cost (convergence allowed), borrower's own contribution ≥10%.

DIGIDHAN Mission → from Union Budget 2017-18; target 2,500 crore digital transactions/yr via UPI/USSD/Aadhaar Pay/IMPS/debit cards; transferred MeitY→Dept of Financial Services, July 2023.

UPI → indigenous digital payment system; transactions grew 92 crore (FY17-18)→13,116 crore (FY23-24), CAGR 129% (cross-ref Batch 5).

SAGY (Saansad Adarsh Gram Yojana) → launched 11 Oct 2014; MPs to make 1 model village/constituency by 2016, +2 more by 2019; Adarsh Gram population 3,000-5,000 (plain)/1,000-3,000 (hilly/tribal/difficult); new-village-identification window ended March 2024.

AYUSH → Dept of Indian System of Medicine & Homeopathy (ISM&H) established 1995 in Min. of Health & Family Welfare; rechristened AYUSH (Ayurveda/Yoga/Naturopathy/Unani/Siddha/Homeopathy) 2003.

Ayushman Bharat-PMJAY → ₹5 lakh/family/yr health cover, secondary+tertiary care, poor/vulnerable; launched 2018, subsumed Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY).

SPMRM (Shyama Prasad Mukherji Rurban Mission) → launched 2016 by Min. of Rural Development; "Rurban clusters" — 25,000-50,000 population (plain/coastal) or 5,000-15,000 (desert/hilly/tribal).

Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) → rural sanitation, eradicate open defecation; renamed from Nirmal Bharat Abhiyan (NBA, w.e.f. 1 Apr 2012) → SBM(G) w.e.f. 2 Oct 2014; Phase II launched 2020-21 (post-ODF) → target: Sampoorna Swachhata + ODF→ODF Plus by 2024-25.

Aadhaar → world's first on-scale digital ID service (formless/paperless); 80crore+ cards issued; world's largest biometric programme; identity infra for welfare-scheme delivery.

DBT → launched 2013 via Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB) + other channels; 35 centrally-sponsored schemes under its purview (LPG/PDS/MGNREGA linked).

DAY-NRLM → flagship poverty-reduction programme, Min. of Rural Development, 2011; world's largest livelihood-improvement initiative; capacity-building focus (trained manpower). AGEY → sub-scheme under DAY-NRLM from FY2017-18. SHGs → community-based women's groups for mutual support/savings/credit. WEAF → under Aajeevika-NRLM, empowers women entrepreneurs in SHGs; loans ≤₹5 lakh/5yrs w/ 2% interest subvention (prompt repayment); also ≤₹1.5 lakh/borrower for ≤3yrs; special focus: "Lakhpati Didis".

Social Sector 7 flagship programmes: Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, Mid-Day Meal Scheme, National Health Mission, Integrated Child Development Services, Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, MGNREGS, 100 Smart Cities.

PMJJBY → started 2015; ₹2 lakh renewable 1-yr life cover, ages 18-50 (cover till 55), any-cause death, premium ₹330/yr. PMSBY → brought 2015; ₹2 lakh accidental-death/disability cover + ₹1 lakh partial-disability, ages 18-70, premium ₹12/yr. APY → launched May 2015; fixed pension ₹1,000/2,000/3,000/4,000/5,000/month from age 60, based on contribution option (entry age 18-40). PMJJBY+PMSBY+APY launched simultaneously at 115 locations.

PMUY → launched 1 May 2016, Ballia UP; LPG connections for BPL households (women's names), replaces unclean cooking fuel; target 5 crore connections. Ujjwala 2.0 → launched 2021; +1 crore additional LPG connections for poor households' adult women.

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana → rooftop solar scheme, launched 13 Feb 2024; solarize 1 crore households; 300 units free electricity/month; ₹75,000 crore investment.

PM Vishwakarma Scheme → launched 17 Sept 2023 (Vishwakarma Jayanti); for traditional artisans (18 identified trades); free registration via CSCs (biometric portal); PM Vishwakarma certificate+ID card; skill upskilling (basic+advanced) + ₹15,000 toolkit incentive; collateral-free credit ≤₹1 lakh (1st tranche)/≤₹2 lakh (2nd tranche) at 5% concessional rate; digital-transaction incentive + marketing support; nodal ministry = MSME.

PM-JANMAN → Pradhan Mantri Janjatiya Unnat Gram Abhiyan — improves PVTG quality of life, 11 critical interventions via 9 line ministries; based on PM-JAN-MAN (launched 2023); India's ST population = 10.45 crore (2011 census); 75 PVTG communities across 18 states + Andaman & Nicobar UT.
