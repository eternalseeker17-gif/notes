Poverty estimation in India: NITI Aayog's task force, using NSSO/MoSPI consumption data. Types: Absolute, Relative, Subjective Poverty. Head Count Ratio = % of population below poverty line. Types of Poor: Usually Poor (chronically BPL), Churning Poor (in/out), Occasionally Poor (usually above line).

HOOK MPI (Multidimensional Poverty Index) — introduced 2010 by UNDP + OPHI; 3 dimensions (Health, Education, Living Standards), 10 indicators. National MPI (NITI Aayog, Nov 2021) uses 12 indicators (adds antenatal care + bank account) — 24.82 crore people escaped multidimensional poverty in 9 years; UP = most people lifted; Bihar = highest MPI (most poor), Kerala = lowest MPI.

HDI — concept by Amartya Sen & Mahbub ul Haq, introduced 1990; score 0-1; 3 dimensions (Life expectancy, Education, GNI per capita PPP$).

2Balance of Payments (BoP) — Structure

Current AccountCapital Account
Trade in goods (visible) + services (invisible) + Transfer Payments (remittances, aid) — India is the top global remittance receiver, Mexico 2ndFDI, FPI, external assistance, loans, asset purchases
HOOK Current Account: receipts > payments = surplus ★ (net lender to the world); payments > receipts = deficit (NOT "balance" — a tested trap, "balance" only when receipts = payments exactly ★). FDI = long-term, ≥10% equity in a foreign physical asset (e.g. Walmart-Flipkart). FPI/FII = short-term financial-asset investment, <10% equity (stocks, bonds) ★ — links Indian markets to global capital post-1991.
HOOK Circular-flow view of trade ★: Imports = leakage (money flows abroad, ↓aggregate demand) ★; Exports = injection (foreign money flows in, ↑aggregate demand) ★ — a directly tested statement pair, both correct.

3The 1991 BoP Crisis

TRAP 1991 crisis = shortage of foreign exchange ★ (not water/electricity/labour). By June 1991, reserves fell to ~$1-1.1 billion ★ — enough for only ~2 weeks of imports ★ (petroleum/essentials). Causes: fiscal deficit, foreign-debt repayment failure, Gulf War oil-price spike, inflation ★ — NOT natural disaster or migration (tested distractors).

India approached IMF and World Bank ★ for a $7 billion loan ★ (via IBRD, part of World Bank Group) — conditions: structural reforms under SAP (Structural Adjustment Programme) — privatisation, trade liberalisation, fiscal discipline, FDI opening ★ → led to LPG reforms, 1991 ★ (Liberalisation, Privatisation, Globalisation), under PM P.V. Narasimha Rao/FM Manmohan Singh.

HOOK Rupee devalued in two stages, July 1991 ★, cumulative ~18-20% against USD ★ — objective: make exports cheaper/competitive, discourage imports, boost forex inflow ★. Devaluation → improved export competitiveness ★ (weaker rupee = cheaper Indian goods abroad — a tested Assertion-Reason, both true, R explains A).

Rupee made fully convertible on Current Account, 19 August 1994 ★ (RBI, under IMF Article VIII).

4Trade Policy — Pre & Post 1991

TRAP 1947-1990 trade policy ★ = Import Substitution (inward-looking, protect domestic industry via high tariffs/quotas — avg import duty >125% ★, govt license mandatory for most imports) — NOT free trade or export-oriented (repeatedly tested distractors across multiple phrasings of the same fact).
Tariff Measures ★Non-Tariff Measures ★
Import taxes, export taxes ★ (both — a tested "which is tariff" question)Anti-dumping measures, Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT), labeling/quality standards ★ (both correct in a tested question)

Bilateral trade agreement ★ — primary goal: promote free trade between two countries ★ (reduce/eliminate customs duties, quotas). FTA (e.g. BIMSTEC) ★ — reduce trade barriers, boost economic cooperation (not common currency or cultural exchange).

5International Institutions

HOOK Bretton Woods Conference, 1944 — 44 nations, 730 delegates → created IMF + World Bank (Twin Organisations); Bretton Woods system collapsed 1971 (US suspended gold convertibility). India ranks 8th in IMF voting power. Top IMF borrowers: Argentina, Egypt, Ukraine, Pakistan, Ecuador.
BodyRole
WTO ★Promotes free & fair trade ★ — does NOT allow arbitrary restrictions (only limited, rule-based protective measures) ★ — a tested Assertion-true/Reason-false pair
IMF ★Loans with Structural Adjustment Programme (SAP) conditions ★ — liberal economic reforms, privatisation, fiscal discipline (used in India's 1991 reforms)
IBRDMain World Bank Group institution — "International Bank for Reconstruction and Development" ★, approached by India in the late 1980s/1991

SAARC Currency Swap Framework (2024-27) ★ — RBI provides short-term liquidity to SAARC nations during forex crises. BIMSTEC Technology Transfer Facility — MoA signed 5th BIMSTEC Summit, Colombo, 30 March 2022 ★; BIMSTEC established 6 June 1997 (Bangkok Declaration), 7 members (Bangladesh, India, Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Thailand, Nepal, Bhutan). SAFTA Article 23 ★ = Dispute Settlement provision (NOT unilateral tariff reservations — a tested Assertion-true/Reason-false).

TermMeaning

Exchange rate evolution: 1928-48 pegged to Pound Sterling → post-1948 pegged to USD (fixed) → 1975 delinked from Pound → 1992 LERMS (dual rate) → 1993 fully market-determined/floating. Tarapore Committee (RBI, 1997, reviewed 2006) — examined Capital Account Convertibility feasibility.

Lorenz Curve — income/wealth distribution graph; Gini Coefficient 0 (perfect equality) to 1 (perfect inequality).
