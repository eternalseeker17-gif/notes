HOOK P.C. Mahalanobis = "Architect of Indian Planning" ★ — birthday 29 June = Statistics Day ★; founded Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata; journal Sankhya. His model prioritised heavy industry/capital goods over agriculture ★ ("machines that make machines"), public sector leading role in steel plants/dams — future production capacity over short-term consumer benefit ★.
TRAP Planning Commission ★ formed 15 March 1950 ★, non-constitutional advisory body, PM = ex-officio Chairman ★ (first Chairman: Nehru ★; first Deputy Chairman: Gulzarilal Nanda ★, PYQ-book-only, see A.2). Replaced by NITI Aayog, 1 Jan 2015 ★ (announced 2014) — "bottom-up"/cooperative-federalism approach. Under the 4th Plan, Indira Gandhi was ex-officio Chairperson ★ (as PM).

Green Revolution ★ introduced during the 3rd Five-Year Plan ★ (1961-66), full effect from 1966-67 (Plan Holiday) — HYV seeds, credited to Dr. M.S. Swaminathan. Import Substitution ★ = inward-looking strategy across the first 7 plans — protect infant domestic industries from foreign competition ★ (NOT to expand trade, attract foreign investors, or create export dependency).

HOOK Hindu Rate of Growth ★ — term for India's slow (~3.5%) growth 1950s-1980s; coined by Prof. Raj Krishna ★.

3Industrial Policy Resolutions (IPR)

IPRYearKey points
1st IPR ★1948 ★First industrial policy of independent India ★; led by Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee ★ (Industry & Commerce Minister); declared Mixed Economy ★; 4 industry categories (Strategic/Basic/Regulated/Other); start of License Raj
2nd IPR ★1956 ★"Economic Constitution of India" ★; objective = growth with equity ★; "socialistic pattern of society" ★; Commanding Heights Doctrine (coined by Lenin, 1922) ★; Schedule A (17 industries, public monopoly ★), Schedule B (12, public+private ★), Schedule C (rest, private) ★ — basis for later Maharatna companies
3rd IPR1977Morarji Desai/Janata govt; SSI emphasis; "Tiny Unit" concept (<₹1 lakh investment); MNC restrictions
4th IPR1980Indira Gandhi govt; back to heavy industries; foreign tech transfer encouraged (FERA 1973); Tiny Unit limit raised to ₹2 lakh
IPR 1991 ★1991Economic Liberalisation ★ — under P.V. Narasimha Rao, reforms by Manmohan Singh; shift from inward-looking to outward-oriented economy
HOOK Karve Committee (1955) ★ — Village & Small-Scale Industries Committee, Planning Commission, chaired by D.G. Karve; promoted rural/labour-intensive small-scale industry ★, NOT large-scale capital-intensive urban manufacturing ★ (a tested Assertion-true/Reason-false).

HOOK India's textile industry ★ is self-reliant across its full value chain ★ (cotton→spinning→weaving→garment→export) — "Farm to Fashion" model. Sugar industry ★: seasonal (sugarcane availability), mills sited near sugarcane fields (sucrose degrades during transport ★); top producers UP/Maharashtra/Karnataka/Tamil Nadu ★; India = 2nd largest sugar producer (after Brazil ★) but 1st in gur/khandsari ★.

Sindri Fertilizer Plant ★ — first major PSU industrial unit of independent India, est. 1951, Dhanbad district, Jharkhand ★ (near Jharia coalfield for fuel supply). Urea = most-produced nitrogenous fertilizer ★; India is import-dependent on potash (not self-sufficient — tested distractor). Chennai = "Detroit of India" ★ (PYQ-book-only, see A.2; auto hub: Hyundai/Ford/BMW/TVS/Ashok Leyland) — NOT a petrochemical hub (that's Jamnagar/Dahej, Gujarat) ★.

61991 Reforms — New Economic Policy (NEP) / LPG

HOOK Reforms launched 24 July 1991 ★ — PM P.V. Narasimha Rao, FM Manmohan Singh ("Rao-Manmohan Model") ★; objective: overcome BoP crisis, dismantle License Raj, integrate globally ★. Also called New Economic Policy (NEP) ★. Divided into: Stabilisation measures (short-term — control inflation ~17%, fix BoP ★, rupee devalued 18-20% July 1991) + Structural reforms (long-term — LPG).
L / P / GMeaning
Liberalisation ★↓govt regulation/licensing/restrictions — removed barriers to firm entry/growth ★ (License Raj restricted entrepreneurial freedom — a tested A-R, both true)
PrivatisationShift PSU ownership/management to private entities; DIPAM (Dept. of Investment & Public Asset Mgmt, ex-Dept of Disinvestment) manages this
Globalisation ★Integration via trade/FDI/tech flow — NOT expanding public-sector control (tested "NOT a goal" distractor) ★
TRAP IMF + World Bank ★ = key reform institutions (loans + policy guidance/structural-reform funding respectively ★) — NOT SAARC+WTO or BRICS+ADB. Washington Consensus does NOT include export subsidies ★ (it advocates trade liberalisation, ending subsidies — a tested "which is NOT part of" question). Trade reform post-1991: removed import quotas, cut tariffs on capital goods, abolished most licensing ★ (kept only for alcohol/cigarettes/hazardous chemicals/explosives — security/health/environment reasons ★).

LERMS (Liberalised Exchange Rate Management System) — dual exchange rate, introduced 1992 ★. Foreign-bank investment limit raised to 74% ★ as part of financial-sector reform. Liberalisation affects all 3 sectors — primary, secondary, tertiary ★ (not just two). National Renewal Fund (Feb 1992, 8th Plan) — worker retraining/VRS support post-1991 reforms ★, abolished 2000.

HOOK Ease of Doing Business (EoDB) ★: "Enforcing Contracts" indicator = time/cost to resolve commercial disputes ★. MCA21 ★ (Ministry of Corporate Affairs e-governance, SPICe+ forms) improved India's "Starting a Business" ranking. DPIIT ★ (Dept. for Promotion of Industry & Internal Trade) = nodal body for industrial promotion + implements PM Gati Shakti ★ (via its Logistics Division). National Infrastructure Pipeline (NIP) ★ — announced 2019-20, formally from 2020 ★, ₹111 lakh crore target (2020-25).
