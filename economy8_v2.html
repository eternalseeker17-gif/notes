2Millets, PMFBY & Commercial Crops

  • International Year of Millets(IYM) → declared by UNGA(75th session, March2021) for 2023; Millets="Smart Food"(high nutrition, climate-resilient, aligned w/ UN SDGs)
  • Commercial crops(def) → produced for trade not self-consumption: Oil-seeds, Sugar crops, Fibre crops, Narcotic crops, Beverage crops
  • ACPC(Agriculture Cost&Price Commission) → decides MSP on govt's behalf
  • MSP(def) → price at which govt is ready to purchase crop from farmers directly, if market price falls below MSP
HOOK PMFBY → launched 2016 Kharif season; comprehensive crop-failure insurance for stabilising farmer income; world's largest crop-insurance scheme by farmer enrolments(~5.5cr applications/yr avg), 3rd-largest by premium received; farmer pays only 1.5%(Rabi)/2%(Kharif) of premium, Centre+State bear rest; 6yrs implementation: farmers paid ₹25,186cr premium, received claims ₹1.2 lakh cr(as of31Oct2022).

4Allied Sectors & NFSA

  • Allied sectors(livestock/forestry&logging/fishing&aquaculture) → buoyant-growth sectors, potential for better farm incomes
  • Livestock sector → CAGR 7.9%(2014-15 to2020-21, constant prices); contribution to agri-GVA ↑24.3%(2014-15)→30.1%(2020-21)
  • Fisheries sector → annual avg growth ~7% since2016-17; ~6.7% share of total agri GVA
  • Dairy sector → most critical livestock component, employs >8cr farmers directly; India ranks 1st in milk production, 3rd in egg production, 8th in meat production globally
HOOK NFSA 2013(notified 10.09.2013) → objective=food&nutritional security via adequate quality food at affordable prices; covers up to 75% rural population + up to 50% urban population(TPDS-subsidised foodgrains) = ~2/3rd of total population; special focus on nutrition for women&children.

5Green Revolution & Agricultural Revolutions

  • White Revolution = speedy ↑ in milk production; Operation Flood started to accelerate it, Father of Operation Flood=Dr.Verghese Kurien; largest integrated dairy-dev programme in the world, started by NDDB in 1970(January1970 per PYQ) ★
  • Yellow Revolution = ↑oil-seeds production | Blue Revolution = progress in fish production
  • 11th Plan(2007-12): agri plan-outlay declined to only 18.5%; agri&allied avg annual GDP growth=3.7%(short of4% target, but better than 10th Plan's 2.4%)
  • Tenth Plan = first plan which did NOT fix crop-production targets
  • Green Revolution did NOT cover barley/ragi/minor-millets; confined to HYV mainly rice/wheat/maize/jowar
  • National Agriculture Insurance Scheme implemented Oct1999

6Land Reforms & Land Holdings

  • Land reorganisation measures: (1) Land ceiling(max land a farmer can hold, excess=illegal), (2) Chakbandi(aggregating divided/broken land parcels)
  • Land-holding classification: Marginal(<1 hectare), Small(1-4 hectares), Large(>4 hectares)
  • Chakbandi first implemented in India in 1920, in Baroda

7Agriculture Finance & NABARD

  • Unorganised finance sources: money-lenders, money-dealers, relatives, businessmen, landlords, commission agents | Organised sources: Co-operative Committees, Co-operative Banks, Commercial Banks, RRBs, the Government
  • Co-operative Credit Organisation started first in 1904; Primary Co-operative Committees=short-period credit; State Co-operative Agriculture&Rural Dev Banks=long-period credit
  • Land Development Bank established 1919(as Land Mortgage Bank); provides long-term loans
HOOK NABARD → apex institution of Rural Credit; established 12 July1982 by merging RBI's Agriculture Credit Dept + Agricultural Refinance&Development Corporation(ARDC); based on Shivraman/Sivaraman Committee recommendations ⚠. Authorised share capital: ₹500cr(original) → ₹5,000cr(w.e.f.1Feb2001) → ₹30,000cr(NABARD Amendment Bill2017, passed January2018; also enabled RBI's exit from NABARD shareholding).

8Green Revolution — Regional Disparity & PYQ Deep-Dive

  • Green Revolution regional disparity ★: HYV/fertilizer/irrigation access initially limited to well-irrigated regions(Punjab/Haryana/Western UP) → rapid growth there, low benefit in rain-fed/less-irrigated areas → regional imbalance
  • A-R ★: Green Revolution led to regional imbalances(TRUE) — because HYV tech+irrigation initially available only in select states(Reason TRUE, correct explanation)
  • 1st phase(mid1960s-mid1970s) effectiveness ★: highly effective in Punjab/Haryana(canal/tube-well irrigation, wheat-centric, HYV seeds); largely INEFFECTIVE in Bihar(flood-prone, limited irrigation, weak infra, small landholdings); Tamil Nadu's impact increased only in later phases(not entirely ineffective)
  • Punjab = first & largest beneficiary state of the Green Revolution ★; Punjab&Haryana → >85% of net sown area under irrigation(Bhakra Nangal canal system+tubewells) ★
TRAP Structural-transformation failure(1950-1990) ★: despite agri's declining GDP share, ~65% of population remained agriculture-dependent by 1990(workforce didn't shift to industry/services as theory predicts) — from ~75%(1950, ~70-75% per another PYQ variant on Independence) to ~65%(1990) after 4 decades of planning.

Verghese Kurien ★: Father of White Revolution in India; led Operation Flood(dairy modernisation, ↑farmer income, milk self-sufficiency); chief architect of Amul model+Anand pattern — NOT the inventor of milk pasteurisation(that was Louis Pasteur, 19th century — tested distractor). Land reforms(post-independence) key equity goal ★: abolition of intermediaries(Zamindari system)+land-ceiling laws → ownership rights to actual cultivators; central principle="land to the tiller"(↑productivity+social justice).

9Agri Schemes, Missions & Institutions — PYQ Deep-Dive

  • Agriculture Infrastructure Fund(AIF) ★ → supports agri-logistics/cold chains/warehouses/primary-processing units; low-interest loans to farmers/FPOs/cooperative societies/agri-entrepreneurs
  • AGMARK ★ → certification mark for agriculture products(quality/purity); issued under Agricultural Produce(Grading&Marking) Act, 1937
  • National Mission on Natural Farming ★ → launched November2024; chemical-free/natural farming for 1 crore farmers; focus=soil health/↓input costs/↑farmer income/sustainable agri
  • Technology Mission on Oilseeds(TMO) ★ → launched 1986 by Ministry of Agriculture; NDDB given oilseed procurement/processing responsibility(cooperative model on 'Operation Flood' lines) → launched 'Operation Golden Flow'(edible-oil self-reliance, remunerative farmer prices)
  • ULPIN(Unique Land Parcel Identification Number) ★ → unique ID for every land parcel in India(TRUE); 14-digit alphanumeric ID(TRUE); tracks ownership/resolves disputes(TRUE); used in BOTH rural AND urban areas(NOT urban-only — a tested FALSE statement)
  • Uttar Pradesh ★ → highest proportion of workforce engaged in agriculture(most populous state, fertile Ganga-Yamuna plain, adequate water, majority-rural population)
  • GMOs in agriculture ★ → main purpose=improve crop yield+pest resistance+nutritional quality(via DNA changes) — eg Bt Cotton(pest resistance), Golden Rice(Vitamin-A enriched) — NOT eliminating fertilizers/pesticides entirely or lab-only cultivation(distractors)
  • Occupational/workforce shift agriculture→industry&services ★ = "occupational diversification"(↓rural-economy risk, new income sources, structural economic change)
  • Fertilizer subsidies(1960-1990) ★ = "double-edged" policy — boosted Green Revolution productivity BUT benefits mainly reaped by richer/large farmers(small farmers under-benefited) → ↑rural economic inequality
  • SMAM(Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization) ★ → FPOs get 75% cost grant for Kisan Drone demonstrations(accessible tech for small farmers, precision agriculture)

Post-independence(~1947) main occupation ★: Agriculture(~70-75% of population dependent), highest GDP contributor(manufacturing/services still nascent). AMUL milk cooperative ★: established 14 December 1946, Anand(Gujarat), under guidance of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel; originally "Kaira District Co-operative Milk Producers' Union Limited"; laid foundation of White Revolution by ending middlemen exploitation.
