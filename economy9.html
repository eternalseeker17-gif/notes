3International Trade Institutions & Agreements

Body/AgreementKey facts ★
GATT ★Signed 1947 ★ — reduced tariffs/trade barriers; replaced by WTO in 1995 ★
GATSExtends trade rules to services — 4 modes (cross-border supply, consumption abroad, commercial presence, movement of natural persons)
Agreement on Agriculture (AoA)Effective 1995, WTO — reduces trade-distorting agri subsidies
HOOK WTO subsidy boxes ★: Green Box = doesn't disrupt trade, no limits; Blue Box = limits production, no limits; Amber Box = disrupts trade, De Minimis limit applies ★.

Anti-dumping duty = on imports priced below normal value; Countervailing duty = offsets export subsidies.
