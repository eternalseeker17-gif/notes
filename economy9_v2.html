2Major Economic Reports & WTO Framework

  • MoSPI reports: PLFS(Periodic Labour Force Survey) by NSO; Monthly Infrastructure Performance Review | RBI reports: Financial Stability Report; Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy | Global Gender Gap Report → WEF
  • WTO → global org regulating international trade, rules-based trade among nations
  • GATT → signed 1947; reduced tariffs/trade barriers; replaced by WTO in 1995
  • GATS(General Agreement on Trade in Services) → extends trade rules to services; 4 modes: cross-border supply, consumption abroad, commercial presence, movement of natural persons
  • AoA(Agreement on Agriculture) → effective 1995(WTO); objective=reduce trade-distorting agri subsidies
  • 3 Subsidy Boxes: Green(don't disrupt trade, no limitations), Blue(limit production, no limitations), Amber(disrupt trade, De Minimis Limit applicable)
  • Trade Protection: Anti-dumping duty(imports priced below normal value); Countervailing duty(offsets export subsidies)

5Key Definitions (Lucent §16)

  • Rules of Origin(WTO) → criteria to determine a product's national source; used for anti-dumping/safeguard measures, MFN-vs-preferential treatment, trade statistics, labelling/marking, govt procurement
  • Forex reserves(per ET) → foreign-currency assets held by central banks: foreign marketable securities+monetary gold+SDRs+reserve position in IMF; purpose=international payments+exchange-rate-risk hedge
  • Infant Mortality Rate(WHO) → probability of a child dying before age1 in a given period(that period's age-specific mortality rates); expressed per1000 live births
  • Human Capital Formation(World Bank) → knowledge/skills/health people invest in&accumulate to realize productive potential; investment in human capital produces more human capital
  • PLI(Production Linked Incentive) → incentives to domestic industries to boost local production
  • Narrow Money(M1) = currency in circulation + demand deposits | Broad Money = M1 + time deposits
  • High Powered Money = currency in circulation w/ public + banks' cash reserves(monetary base; CRR=fraction of it, set by central bank)
  • Gross Primary Deficit = Gross Fiscal Deficit − interest payments | Net Primary Deficit = Net Fiscal Deficit − net interest payments(interest paid−interest receipt)
  • Fiscal Deficit(def) = total revenue − total expenditure of govt; indicates total borrowings needed; borrowings NOT included in total revenue

6Global Peace, Gender Gap & Happiness — PYQ Deep-Dive

HOOK Global Peace Index(GPI) ★, published by Institute for Economics and Peace(IEP, Sydney-based): "Positive Peace" = institutions/attitudes/structures sustaining a peaceful society(NOT just absence of violence) — identifies structural factors promoting peace, can forecast peacefulness declines; high-positive-peace countries=more economically stable. GPI2024 trend since2008 ★ = mixed: "Ongoing Conflict&Societal Safety/Security" deteriorated, "Militarisation" improved. GPI2025(19th edition) ★: global peace declined 0.36%(gradual decline — militarisation/internal conflict/geopolitical tensions); Iceland=most peaceful continuously since2008(incl.2025); India ranked 115th(improved from116th).
  • Global Gender Gap Report(WEF) ★ → 4 key areas: political empowerment, economic participation&opportunity, educational attainment, health&survival; GGGR2025: Northern America leads in closing gender gap; 'Economic Participation&Opportunity' dimension covers women's company-board/senior-role representation ★
  • World Happiness Report ★ → UN SDSN(since2012, 1st report 1Apr2012)+Gallup World Poll; 6 factors: GDP per capita, social support, healthy life expectancy, freedom to make life choices, generosity, perception of corruption; primary dependent variable="life satisfaction"(Cantril Ladder,0-10); annual, usually 20 March(Int'l Day of Happiness); WHR2025: India=118th/147(improved from126th); Finland=top for consecutive yrs; Taiwan=happiest in Asia(2025)
TRAP A-R pairs ★: (1) India's Happiness/GHI rank lower than most South Asian neighbours(TRUE, incl Nepal/Bhutan/SriLanka) — but NOT because India's GDP per capita is lowest in South Asia(Reason FALSE, Afghanistan's is lower). (2) Happiness Index includes political freedom&social support(TRUE) — but published by UN-SDSN NOT UNDP(Reason FALSE). NOT-included Happiness-Index factors ★: sports performance, internet speed, currency value, tax collection rate, population-growth monitoring.

Happiness Index promotes citizen-centric governance approach ★(mental well-being/health/social security focus, not just GDP).

10HDI & MPI — PYQ Deep-Dive

HOOK HDI ★ → released by UNDP, first introduced 1990(economists Mahbub-ul-Haq+Amartya Sen); 3 dimensions: health(life expectancy at birth), education(mean years of schooling+expected years of schooling), standard of living(per-capita GNI) ★. Expected Years of Schooling = years a school-entry-age child is expected to spend in school given current enrolment patterns ★. Mean Years of Schooling = avg yrs of schooling for adults ≥25yrs ★. Main limitation ★ = fails to capture social inequality/unequal income distribution(reflects only national averages). "Capability approach" criticism ★ = intangible capabilities(freedom/self-respect/life-choices) hard to measure → HDI stays limited to health/education/income.
  • A-R ★: HDI can ↑ even if income drops(TRUE) — improvements in life expectancy&education can offset income decline, since HDI=average of 3 dimensions(Reason TRUE, correct explanation)
  • A-R ★: high-HDI country may still have high multidimensional poverty(TRUE) — HDI is an average, doesn't capture intra-country inequality(Reason TRUE, correct explanation)
  • A-R ★: India's HDI improved post-2010(TRUE) — Reason: methodology revised to include Inequality-adjusted HDI(IHDI) in 2010(TRUE, but NOT the correct explanation — actual improvement driven by domestic development/social-welfare schemes, not the methodology revision)
  • Statement check ★: HDI is NOT based on life expectancy+standard of living ONLY(also includes education, statement1 INCORRECT); life expectancy at birth IS used(statement2 CORRECT); MPI does NOT measure health+education ONLY(also standard of living, statement3 INCORRECT); child mortality+nutrition ARE MPI health indicators(statement4 CORRECT) → only statements 2&4 correct
  • "Human Development Ethics" critique ★: development shouldn't be limited to quantitative data alone, should incorporate cultural diversity+ethical values — challenges HDI's implicit universal/Western-values assumption
