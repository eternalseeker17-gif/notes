1Classical Dances (8 per Sangeet Natak Akademi + Chhau)

DanceStateElement ★Founder/Reviver & key facts
Bharatanatyam ★Tamil Nadu ★FireOldest classical dance ★; solo (Ekaharya); earlier Sadirattam/Dasiattam ★ (performed by Devadasis); revived by E. Krishna Iyer & Rukmini Devi Arundale; based on Natya Shastra (Bharat Muni) + Abhinaya Darpana (Nandikeshvara) ★; three elements Nritta/Nritya/Natya ★; Alarippu = invocatory, pure nritta, "blossoming of a flower" ★; Padam = love/divine affection (shringara rasa) ★
Kuchipudi ★Andhra PradeshEarthEarlier Bhagavata Mela Dance; developed by Tirtha Narayanayati & Siddhendra Yogi → Bhama Kalpam; combines dance+mime+acting, confluence of Nritya/Nritta/Natya ★; concluding item Tarangam danced on rim of a brass plate
Odissi ★Odisha ★WaterGotipua ★ = precursor (young boys, precede classical Odissi); father of Odissi = Kelucharan Mohapatra; two foundational postures ★: Chauka (square, stable, Tandava/masculine) & Tribhanga (three-bend, Lasya/feminine)
Kathak ★Uttar Pradesh ★ (Northern India)—Only classical dance with Persian elements, Mughal-patronised; "Katha" = storytelling ★; Gharanas: Lucknow (Ishwari Prasad), Jaipur (Bhanuji Maharaj), Banaras (Janaki Prasad); organised development via Kathakars in Bhakti period (15th–16th c.)
Mohiniyattam ★KeralaAirVishnu's female avatar "Mohini"; revived by King Swathi Thirunal ★ (also inculcated Carnatic music into it); "Dance of Enchanters", 24 hand gestures
Kathakali ★Kerala ★—Katha=story, Kali=performance; male performers, masked ★, face-painted (green=strength/noble, red=evil); based on Indian epics — Ramayana/Mahabharata/Bhagavata Purana ★, script called Attakatha ★; NOT silent — singers (Ponnani/Singidi) sing from behind stage ★; five Veshams: Pacha, Kathi, Thadi, Kari, Minukku
Manipuri ★Manipur ★—Based on Vaishnavism, theme = devotion/love via Raslila (Krishna-Gopis) ★; also known as Jagoi ★; revived in early 20th c. by Rabindranath Tagore via Shantiniketan ★ (NOT Uday Shankar/Rukmini Devi/Kelucharan); Sankirtana tradition (Jagoi+Cholom) inscribed UNESCO ICH 2013 ★; martial form = Thang-Ta (sword+spear) ★
Sattriya ★Assam ★—Introduced 15th century by Srimanta Sankaradeva ★ (Vaishnav saint/reformer); originated from Satras (Vaishnav monasteries) ★; theme = devotion to Krishna ★; included as classical dance category in 2000
Chhau ★Odisha/Jharkhand/West Bengal ★ (Eastern India)—Syncretic — merges tribal, folk & classical Hindu elements ★; three styles ★: Seraikella (Jharkhand, masks used), Mayurbhanj (Odisha, NO masks), Purulia (West Bengal, large colourful masks); recognised as cultural heritage by UNICEF and UNESCO ★ (NOT in the UNESCO Representative ICH List the way Kalbelia/Baul/Sankirtana are — book distinguishes "recognised as heritage" from the formal list)
TRAP Chronological order of earliest historical references (oldest→newest) ★: Bharatanatyam → Kathak → Manipuri → Mohiniyattam — a directly tested ordering question.
HOOK Sangeet Natak Akademi recognises 8 classical dances; Ministry of Culture recognises 9 (adds Chhau). The book does not print an explicit named list of the 8 — inferred from the individual sections: Bharatanatyam, Kuchipudi, Odissi, Kathak, Mohiniyattam, Kathakali, Manipuri, Sattriya.
