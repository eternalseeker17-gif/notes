Rashtriya Yuva Sashaktikaran Karyakram (RYSK)Umbrella scheme merging youth schemes: Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan (NYKS, launched 1972), National Youth Corps (NYC, 2010–11), NPYAD (2008, includes Tenzing Norgay National Adventure Awards), Youth Hostels, Scouting & Guiding assistance, National Young Leaders Programme (NYLP, 2014)
National Service Scheme (NSS) ★Introduced 1969; motto = "Not Me, But You" ★ (Mahatma Gandhi's ideology); "Education through service"
