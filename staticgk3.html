A.2 — PYQ-book-only facts (not verified against the raw extraction)

  • The specific detail that Bhagoria begins exactly "7 days before Holi" and Mattu Pongal specifically "adorns" cattle are PYQ-book framings; the raw extraction confirms the festivals and general timing but not these exact phrasings.
  • Goncha's founder as "Maharaja Purushottam Dev" (visiting Jagannath Puri) is the PYQ book's claim, conflicting with the raw extraction's "Chalukya rulers" attribution — flagged as a cross-book doubt in §5.
  • The Chithirai Festival (Madurai, Lord Kallazhagar) is not covered anywhere in the raw extraction at all — entirely PYQ-book-only.
  • Sankranti's exact day-names ("Pedda Panduga", "Mukkanuma") and Wangala's deity name "Misi Saljong" are PYQ-book-only detail beyond what the raw extraction's Andhra Pradesh/Meghalaya blocks name.
  • Puthari's deity "Lord Iguthappa" and Kharchi Puja's deity-group name "Chaturdasha Devta" are PYQ-book-only terms — the raw extraction names the Kodava festivals and the "14 deities" count but not these specific names.
  • Rajni Gerba (Dimasa community, Assam) is not in the raw extraction at all — entirely PYQ-book-only.

A.3 — Doubts carried forward from the raw extraction

Bonalu "started in 1813" (as printed in both sources, corroborated). Raulane Festival's "5,000-year-old" age claim (Himachal, raw extraction A.8) is unverified — not tested in this PYQ set. Ganesh Chaturthi's "Krishna Paksha of Bhado" in Lucent (commonly Shukla Paksha) — not resolved, not tested here. Barawafat's date/meaning differs between Parmar (Eid-e-Milad, birth) and Lucent (death anniversary) — not tested in this PYQ set.
