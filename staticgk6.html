1Ancient Sculpture Schools — Gandhara vs Mathura

Gandhara ★Mathura ★
Character ★Realistic — materialistic/rationalist ★Idealistic — spiritual/emotionalist ★
Influence ★Greek/Roman ★ ("Indo-Greek"/"Greco-Buddhist"), Buddha resembles Apollo (curly hair, muscular) ★Almost free of foreign influence ★, carries forward Sanchi-Bharhut style
MaterialGrey stone/slateRed sandstone (spotted) ★
Subject/theme ★Buddha & Bodhisattva (mostly Maitreya) ★Buddha, Bodhisattva, Jain Tirthankaras, Hindu deities ★ — indigenous iconography ★
Patron ★Saka-Kushana, climax under Kanishka ★Saka-Kushana; Kanishka also patronised its development ★
FeatureHalo plain/non-decorated (Gandhara's key contribution = making of the Halo) ★Fleshy figures, direct gaze, slight smile, natural proportions, elegant human figures ★
TRAP "Which school had MORE Hellenistic influence" ★ = Gandhara — NOT Mathura (a directly tested exclusion). Bodhisattva statues at Gandhara show Greek influence ★, not Persian/Rajput/Mughal.
HOOK Ancient Indian metal-casting technique = Cire-perdue (Lost-Wax Casting) ★ — wax model coated in clay, heated to melt out wax, molten metal poured in. Examples: Indus Valley "Dancing Girl", Chola "Nataraja" statues ★.

2Rajput & Colonial Architecture

TermMeaning ★
Jharokha ★Overhanging balcony/window projecting from a wall in Rajput/Rajasthani architecture ★ — light/ventilation, confidential observation, decoration (Hawa Mahal Jaipur, Mehrangarh Fort Jodhpur) ★
Gothic architecture ★Originated in Europe ★; pointed arches, ribbed vaults, height/vastness, stained glass — used in colonial India (Mumbai's CSMT) ★
HOOK Mumbai's Victorian Gothic + Art Deco heritage ★ (Oval Maidan precinct — UNESCO listed): Victorian Gothic buildings (Bombay High Court, CSMT) show Neo-Gothic elements adapted with sloping tiled roofs/verandahs for Indian climate ★, built using local black basalt + Porbandar limestone ★ (a tested Assertion-Reason pair where the local-materials Reason correctly explains the climate-adaptation Assertion) ★. Oval Maidan separates 19th-c. Victorian Gothic (east) from 20th-c. Art Deco (west) buildings ★ — Art Deco was NOT part of 19th-century colonial development (a tested false statement) ★.
HOOK Puducherry ★ = known for abundance of French colonial architecture ★ (White Town: yellow-walled villas, French balconies, "Franco-Tamil" style). Notre Dame des Anges Church, Puducherry ★: blend of French Classical + Greco-Roman, Doric & Corinthian columns ★, described as a "transparent enclosure with pillars" ★ — NOT a Shikhara/minaret/corbelled-arch feature (distractors).

3Mughal Architecture

Blend of Persian, Turkish, Islamic and Indian styles — unique Indo-Islamic aesthetic ★ (visible in Taj Mahal, Humayun's Tomb). Primary materials: red sandstone (Red Fort, Fatehpur Sikri) and white marble (Taj Mahal) — both correct, not either/or ★.

Monument/StyleFact ★
Khajuraho Temple ★Nagara style ★ (NOT French influence) — built by Chandela rulers, Panchayatana style, high Shikharas ★
Qutub Minar ★Indo-Islamic style ★ — started by Qutubuddin Aibak, blend of arches and geometric carvings
India GateEdwardian Baroque/Triumphal Arch — designed by Edwin Lutyens as WWI memorial
Lotus TempleModern style — designed by Iranian architect Fariborz Sahba, a Baha'i House of Worship

4Temple Architecture — Terms & Named Monuments

TermMeaning ★
Mandapa ★Pillared hall in front of the Garbhagriha, for worshippers/rituals/congregations ★ — NOT a gateway/wall/water tank (distractors); larger temples may have ardha-mandapa, maha-mandapa
Latina (temple roof)Rectangular/wagon-shaped base, curved shikhara narrowing to a point at kalash
PhamsanaShorter/broader than Latina; roof of slabs rising to a single point on a straight slope
ValabhiRectangular base, vaulted-chamber-shaped roof
TempleFact ★
Sanchi Stupa ★Mauryan structure famous for its 4 carved gateways (toranas) ★ — Buddha's life & Jataka tales depicted; Raisen district, MP
Mahabodhi Temple ★Bodh Gaya, Bihar ★ — site of Buddha's enlightenment (Peepal/Bodhi tree); UNESCO World Heritage 2002 ★; blend of early Dravidian + Gupta architecture
Konark Sun Temple ★Shaped as a divine chariot with 7 horses ★ (representing the 7 days of the week) and 24 wheels (24 hours); built 13th c. by Narasimhadeva I, Eastern Ganga dynasty
Modhera Sun Temple ★Key feature = Surya Kund, a huge stepped rectangular reservoir ★ with 108 miniature shrines — NOT Gopuram/Garuda pillar/Vimana (distractors); Mehsana district, Gujarat; built 1026-27 by Bhimdev I, Solanki dynasty
Rumtek Monastery ★Largest/most famous monastery in Sikkim ★, known for Thangka murals + annual Cham (mask) dance ★

5UNESCO World Heritage Sites (monument-specific, PYQ-tested)

SiteFact ★
Hampi ★Vijayanagara style of architecture ★ — capital of Vijayanagara Empire, UNESCO 1986 ★ — NOT Pattadakal (Chalukyan, Dravida+Nagara blend, UNESCO 1987 ★, a tested distractor pair)
Sanchi Stupa ★See §4 above — 4 toranas, Buddha's life/Jataka tales ★
Mahabodhi Temple ★See §4 above — Bihar, 2002 ★
Basilica of Bom Jesus ★Goa, part of "Churches and Convents of Old Goa" ★, UNESCO 1986; Indo-Portuguese style ★ — NOT Indo-French (a tested exclusion vs. Auroville Matrimandir/Chandannagar) ★; houses remains of St. Francis Xavier
Khajuraho Temples ★Erotic sculptures ★, Nagara style, built by Chandela kings 10th-11th c., depict the four Purusharthas (Dharma/Artha/Kama/Moksha) and Yoga-Bhoga harmony ★
Chilika Lake ★India's first Ramsar site (1981) ★ — Odisha, Asia's largest & world's 2nd-largest brackish lagoon ★; habitat for migratory birds and endangered Irrawaddy dolphin — NOT a UNESCO World Heritage Site per se in this PYQ's framing, but the tested "Ramsar wetland" answer among the options ★
DOUBT Chilika Lake is a Ramsar Wetland of International Importance, a distinct international designation from UNESCO World Heritage — the PYQ book lists it under a "UNESCO/heritage" bucket alongside genuine UNESCO WHS entries (Hampi, Mahabodhi, Basilica of Bom Jesus, Khajuraho); this batch keeps the PYQ's own grouping but flags the designation difference here for accuracy.

A.3 — Doubts carried forward from the raw extraction

Kandariya Mahadev's printed construction date "1209 AD" conflicts with Chandela king Dhanga's known reign (c.950–1002) — flagged in the raw extraction, not tested in this PYQ batch. Dashawatar Temple (Deogarh) is called both "first example of Panchayatan/Shikhara style" (footnote) and "first example of Nagar style" (main text) in the raw extraction — not reconciled, not tested here.
