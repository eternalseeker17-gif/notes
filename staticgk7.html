A.3 — Doubts carried forward from the raw extraction

Several internal date conflicts flagged in the raw extraction, none tested here: Kargil Vijay Diwas/World Hepatitis Day/International Tiger Day appear duplicated under both July and September in Parmar (likely a printing slip); Odisha's "Utkal Day 1939" (commonly dated 1936 elsewhere); National Integration Day placed under 19 December in Parmar despite Indira Gandhi's birthday being 19 November; Good Governance Day's printed Vajpayee birth year "1954" (actually 1924); World Asthma Day listed as 11 December in Lucent (usually observed in May). None of these are tested in this PYQ batch.
