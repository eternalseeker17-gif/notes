6Places & Buildings Associated with Eminent Persons

ItemFact
India Gate ★Architect Edwin Lutyens ★ (1931, All India War Memorial for WWI + Afghan War martyrs); Lutyens also designed Rashtrapati Bhavan (hence "Lutyens' Delhi") and (jointly with Herbert Baker) Sansad Bhawan — NOT Le Corbusier/Frank Lloyd Wright/Antoni Gaudí (tested distractors)
Mario de Miranda ★Cartoonist, hailed from Goa ★ — known for work in The Illustrated Weekly of India and The Times of India, and the character "Miss Fonseca" — NOT Kerala/Maharashtra/Karnataka (tested distractors)
